# 🔥 Common Mistakes to Watch

## 1. Not Reading the Complete Sentence

❌ Looking only at the blank.

### Fix

Always read:

```text
before blank + blank + after blank
```

The strongest clue may appear **after** the blank.

---

## 2. Ignoring Contrast Words

Common signals:

```text
but
although
however
despite
yet
whereas
nevertheless
```

### Fix

Whenever you see one, immediately think:

> **"The sentence is probably changing direction."**

---

## 3. Choosing a Grammatically Correct Option

A word may fit grammatically but fail logically.

### Fix

Ask:

```text
Does it fit grammar?
AND
Does it fit meaning?
```

Both must be YES.

---

## 4. Ignoring Collocations

Example:

```text
take a decision
make a decision
```

Depending on context and variety of English, both may occur, but placement questions usually expect the standard collocation used by the test.

Other important patterns:

```text
take responsibility
make an effort
pay attention
conduct research
gain experience
achieve a goal
```

---

## 5. Solving Double Blanks Independently

❌ Don't do:

```text
Blank 1 → looks correct
Blank 2 → looks correct
```

Instead:

```text
Blank 1 + Blank 2 + sentence relationship
```

---

## 6. Ignoring Word Form

Example:

> She is very _____.

Possible:

```text
skill
skilled
skillfully
skills
```

Correct:

```text
skilled
```

Because the blank needs an adjective.

---

## 7. Confusing Similar Words

Maintain a personal list:

```text
affect / effect
advice / advise
accept / except
principal / principle
compliment / complement
stationary / stationery
```

Add new confusing pairs whenever you encounter them.

# 🏆 Golden Rule

> **Don't memorize the answer. Memorize the reason the answer is correct.**

That is what makes fill-in-the-blanks questions easier when the options change.
