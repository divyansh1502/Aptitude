# 3. Grammar Signals

## Subject-Verb

```text
each/every/everyone → singular verb
one of + plural noun → singular verb
a number of + plural noun → plural verb
the number of + plural noun → singular verb
```

---

## Tense Signals

```text
yesterday / last week / ago → simple past

since / for + continuing action → often present perfect

already / just / yet → often present perfect

by the time + past event → often past perfect
```

---

## Article Signals

```text
a → consonant sound
an → vowel sound
the → specific/known noun
no article → general plural/uncountable noun in many contexts
```

Examples:

```text
a university
an hour
a European
an honest person
```

# 4. Word Forms

Identify what type of word the blank requires.

### After "is/am/are/was/were"

Usually:

```text
adjective
```

Example:

> She is ______.

→ confident

---

### Before a noun

Usually:

```text
adjective / determiner
```

> a ______ decision

→ difficult

---

### After "to"

Usually:

```text
base verb
```

> to ______

→ improve

---

### After a modal

```text
can
could
may
might
shall
should
will
would
must
```

Use:

```text
modal + base verb
```

Example:

> He can ______ the problem.

✅ solve

❌ solves

---

### After a preposition

Usually:

```text
noun / pronoun / gerund
```

Example:

> She is interested in ______.

✅ learning

# 8. Comparison Signals

```text
although → contrast
but → contrast
however → contrast
unlike → difference
whereas → contrast
while → contrast/comparison
similarly → similarity
likewise → similarity
```

### Example

> Unlike his brother, Rahul is ______.

The blank should describe a difference.

# 9. Double Blank Shortcut

For:

```text
_____ ______
```

Use:

```text
BLANK 1
   ↓
BLANK 2
   ↓
RELATIONSHIP
```

### If "but/however" appears:

```text
Blank 1 ↔ Blank 2 = CONTRAST
```

### If "and/also" appears:

```text
Blank 1 ↔ Blank 2 = SIMILAR/SUPPORTING
```

### If "because" appears:

```text
Blank 1 ← REASON → Blank 2
```

# 10. Elimination Formula

When unsure:

```text
Remove grammar mistakes
        ↓
Remove wrong word forms
        ↓
Remove wrong tense
        ↓
Remove wrong meaning
        ↓
Check collocation
        ↓
Choose
```

# 11. Quick Cheat Sheet

### 🔥 Five Things to Check

```text
1. Grammar
2. Clue word
3. Tone

```

### 🔥 Most Important Signals

```text
BUT        → opposite
ALTHOUGH   → opposite
DESPITE    → opposite
BECAUSE    → reason
THEREFORE  → result
AND        → addition
UNLIKE     → difference
SIMILARLY  → similarity
```

### 🔥 Final Rule

> **Never choose an option because it merely fits grammatically. Choose the option that fits grammar + context + meaning.**
