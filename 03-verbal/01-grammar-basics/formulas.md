# 1. Subject–Verb Agreement

### Core Rule

```text
Singular Subject → Singular Verb
Plural Subject   → Plural Verb
```

```text
He works.
They work.
```

### High-Yield Rules

| Pattern        | Rule                       | Example                                           |
| -------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Each           | Singular                   | Each student **is** ready.                        |
| Every          | Singular                   | Every student **has** a book.                     |
| Neither        | Singular                   | Neither answer **is** correct.                    |
| Either         | Singular                   | Either option **is** acceptable.                  |
| A + along with | Main subject controls verb | Ram, along with his friends, **is** coming.       |
| A + as well as | Main subject controls verb | The teacher, as well as students, **is** present. |
| Either...or    | Usually nearer subject     | Either the teacher or students **are** coming.    |
| Neither...nor  | Usually nearer subject     | Neither the teacher nor students **are** ready.   |

### Hidden Subject Trap

```text
The quality of the products is good.
```

Subject = `quality`

Not:

```text
products
```

### Remember

> **Ignore the words between the subject and the verb.**

# 2. Tenses

## Basic Formula Table

| Tense                      | Formula                | Example                        |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Present Simple             | V1 / V1+s              | He **works**.                  |
| Present Continuous         | is/am/are + V-ing      | He **is working**.             |
| Present Perfect            | has/have + V3          | He **has worked**.             |
| Present Perfect Continuous | has/have been + V-ing  | He **has been working**.       |
| Past Simple                | V2                     | He **worked**.                 |
| Past Continuous            | was/were + V-ing       | He **was working**.            |
| Past Perfect               | had + V3               | He **had worked**.             |
| Past Perfect Continuous    | had been + V-ing       | He **had been working**.       |
| Future Simple              | will + V1              | He **will work**.              |
| Future Continuous          | will be + V-ing        | He **will be working**.        |
| Future Perfect             | will have + V3         | He **will have worked**.       |
| Future Perfect Continuous  | will have been + V-ing | He **will have been working**. |

### Time Clues

```text
every day       → Present Simple
now             → Present Continuous
already/yet     → Present Perfect
since/for       → Perfect / Perfect Continuous
yesterday       → Past Simple
last week       → Past Simple
ago             → Past Simple
before + past   → Past Perfect
tomorrow        → Future
by + future     → Future Perfect
```

### Since vs For

```text
Since → Starting point
For   → Duration
```

```text
since 2020
since Monday

for two years
for three hours
```

### Perfect Tense Trap

```text
I finished the work yesterday.       ✅
I have finished the work yesterday.  ❌
```

A finished specific past time generally requires the simple past.

# 3. Articles

## A / An / The

```text
A   → consonant SOUND
An  → vowel SOUND
The → specific / known / unique
```

### Examples

```text
a book
a university
a European

an apple
an hour
an honest man
```

### Important

> **Choose `a/an` according to sound, not spelling.**

```text
a university    ✅
an hour         ✅
```

### First vs Second Mention

```text
I saw a dog.
The dog was black.
```

```text
First/general → A/AN
Specific/known → THE
```

### Superlatives

```text
the best
the fastest
the most useful
```

### Unique Things

```text
the Sun
the Earth
the Moon
```

### Zero Article

General plural / uncountable nouns often take no article:

```text
Dogs are loyal.
Water is essential.
Information is useful.
```

# 4. Prepositions

## Time

```text
AT → exact time
ON → day/date
IN → month/year/long period
```

```text
at 5 PM
on Monday
on 15 August
in January
in 2026
```

## Place

```text
AT → point/location
ON → surface
IN → enclosed area
```

```text
at the station
on the table
in the room
```

## Since vs For

```text
since 2020
since Monday

for two years
for three hours
```

## By vs With

```text
BY   → agent / means
WITH → instrument
```

```text
The book was written by him.
I travelled by train.

He cut the fruit with a knife.
```

## Common Fixed Prepositions

```text
good at
interested in
depend on
responsible for
afraid of
proud of
famous for
different from
similar to
capable of
consist of
belong to
```

### Exam Trick

> **Prepositions are often tested through fixed word combinations.**

# 5. Pronouns

## Subject vs Object

```text
Subject → I, he, she, we, they
Object  → me, him, her, us, them
```

```text
She and I went there.       ✅
They called him.            ✅
```

### Reflexive Pronouns

```text
I       → myself
you     → yourself
he      → himself
she     → herself
we      → ourselves
they    → themselves
```

Use when subject and object refer to the same entity:

```text
He hurt himself.
She introduced herself.
```

### Common Trap

```text
Ram and I completed the work.       ✅
Ram and myself completed the work.  ❌
```

### Who vs Whom

```text
Who   → Subject
Whom  → Object
```

Shortcut:

```text
he → who
him → whom
```

```text
Who called you?
Whom did you meet?
```

# 6. Conjunctions

## FANBOYS

```text
F → For
A → And
N → Nor
B → But
O → Or
Y → Yet
S → So
```

### Correlative Conjunctions

```text
either...or
neither...nor
both...and
not only...but also
whether...or
```

### Although

```text
Although + clause, main clause
```

Avoid:

```text
Although he was tired, but he worked. ❌
```

Use:

```text
Although he was tired, he worked. ✅
```

### Unless

```text
Unless ≈ If...not
```

```text
You will fail unless you study.
```

≈

```text
You will fail if you do not study.
```

# 7. Adjectives & Adverbs

## Adjective

Describes a:

```text
Noun / Pronoun
```

```text
a careful student
a difficult question
```

## Adverb

Modifies:

```text
Verb / Adjective / Adverb
```

```text
He runs quickly.
She is extremely smart.
```

### Comparison

```text
Positive → tall
Comparative → taller
Superlative → tallest
```

```text
Two → Comparative
Three or more → Superlative
```

### Never Double Compare

```text
more better     ❌
more faster     ❌
most fastest    ❌
```

Correct:

```text
better
faster
fastest
```

# 8. Countable & Uncountable Nouns

| Countable | Uncountable |
| --------- | ----------- |
| students  | information |
| books     | advice      |
| questions | furniture   |
| mistakes  | knowledge   |
| apples    | money       |
| cars      | news        |

### Many vs Much

```text
Many → Countable
Much  → Uncountable
```

```text
many questions
much information
```

### Fewer vs Less

```text
Fewer → Countable
Less  → Uncountable
```

```text
fewer mistakes
less time
```

### Common Uncountable Traps

```text
information   ❌ informations
advice        ❌ advices
furniture     ❌ furnitures
```

Use:

```text
a piece of information
a piece of advice
a piece of furniture
```

# 9. Commonly Confused Words

| Word      | Meaning         | Example                             |
| --------- | --------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| its       | possession      | The company changed **its** policy. |
| it's      | it is / it has  | **It's** raining.                   |
| their     | possession      | **Their** project is ready.         |
| there     | place/existence | The book is **there**.              |
| they're   | they are        | **They're** working.                |
| your      | possession      | **Your** laptop                     |
| you're    | you are         | **You're** right.                   |
| then      | sequence/time   | First study, **then** practice.     |
| than      | comparison      | Faster **than** him.                |
| affect    | usually verb    | It **affects** performance.         |
| effect    | usually noun    | It has an **effect**.               |
| principal | main/person     | School principal                    |
| principle | rule/belief     | Moral principle                     |
| advice    | noun            | He gave me advice.                  |
| advise    | verb            | I advise you to practice.           |

# 10. Error-Spotting Order

When you see a grammar question, check in this order:

```text
1. SUBJECT
      ↓
2. VERB / AGREEMENT
      ↓
3. TENSE
      ↓
4. ARTICLE
      ↓
5. PREPOSITION
      ↓
6. PRONOUN
      ↓
7. CONJUNCTION
      ↓
8. ADJECTIVE / ADVERB
      ↓
9. WORD USAGE
      ↓
10. MEANING
```

### The Golden Rule

> **Don't choose an answer because it "sounds right". Identify the rule first.**

Grammar-based placement questions repeatedly target these same categories: agreement, tense, articles, prepositions, pronouns, modifiers/comparison, and common usage.
