# Part A — Representative Questions

## Q1. Subject–Verb Agreement

**Choose the correct sentence.**

A. The list of selected candidates are on the notice board.
B. The list of selected candidates is on the notice board.
C. The list of selected candidates were on the notice board.
D. The list of selected candidates have been on the notice board.

### Answer

**B. The list of selected candidates is on the notice board.**

### Explanation

The true subject is **list**, not **candidates**.

> The list **is**...

The phrase **“of selected candidates”** only gives additional information about the list.

### Rule

**Ignore the prepositional phrase between the subject and verb.**

---

## Q2. Tenses

**Choose the correct option.**

By the time we reached the station, the train ______.

A. leaves
B. has left
C. had left
D. left

### Answer

**C. had left**

### Explanation

There are two events in the past:

1. Train left.
2. We reached the station.

The train leaving happened **before** another past event.

Therefore, use **past perfect**:

> The train **had left**.

### Rule

**Earlier past event → Past Perfect**

---

## Q3. Articles

**Choose the correct option.**

He is ______ honest employee.

A. a
B. an
C. the
D. no article

### Answer

**B. an**

### Explanation

“Honest” begins with a vowel **sound** because the `h` is silent.

> an honest employee

### Rule

Use **a/an according to pronunciation, not spelling.**

---

## Q4. Prepositions

**Choose the correct option.**

She has been working here ______ 2023.

A. for
B. from
C. since
D. by

### Answer

**C. since**

### Explanation

**2023** is a specific point in time.

> since + point of time

Examples:

* since Monday
* since 2023
* since morning

Compare:

> for + duration → for three years

---

## Q5. Parallelism

**Choose the grammatically correct sentence.**

A. She likes reading, writing, and to travel.
B. She likes to read, writing, and travelling.
C. She likes reading, writing, and travelling.
D. She likes read, write, and travelling.

### Answer

**C. She likes reading, writing, and travelling.**

### Explanation

All three items follow the same grammatical structure:

* reading
* writing
* travelling

This is called **parallel structure**.

### Rule

When items are joined in a list, keep their grammatical forms consistent.

# Part D — Quick Revision

## 🔥 15 Rules to Remember

1. **Each / Every → singular verb**
2. **A number of → plural verb**
3. **The number of → singular verb**
4. **Either...or / Neither...nor → nearer subject**
5. **Along with / as well as → doesn't change the subject**
6. **Since → point of time**
7. **For → duration**
8. **At → exact time**
9. **On → day/date**
10. **In → month/year/longer period**
11. **A/An → based on sound**
12. **Information / advice / equipment → uncountable**
13. **One of the + plural noun**
14. **Comparative → -er / more**
15. **Superlative → -est / most**
