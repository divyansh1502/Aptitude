## Part A: 5 Representative Questions (with solutions)

### Q1. Subject-Verb Agreement
Choose the correct option: *The list of items ___ on the table.*

(A) are  (B) is  (C) were  (D) have been

**Solution:** The subject is **list** (singular). *Of items* is only a phrase and is ignored.
**Answer: (B) is**

---

### Q2. Tense
Choose the correct option: *She ___ in Pune since 2019.*

(A) lives  (B) lived  (C) has lived  (D) is living

**Solution:** *Since 2019* is a signal for the present perfect, which links the past to now.
**Answer: (C) has lived**

---

### Q3. Articles
Choose the correct option: *He is ___ honest man.*

(A) a  (B) an  (C) the  (D) no article

**Solution:** *Honest* begins with a vowel **sound** (silent h).
**Answer: (B) an**

---

### Q4. Prepositions
Choose the correct option: *The meeting is scheduled ___ Monday.*

(A) at  (B) in  (C) on  (D) by

**Solution:** Days of the week take **on**.
**Answer: (C) on**

---

### Q5. Pronouns
Choose the correct option: *Between you and ___, the plan is risky.*

(A) I  (B) me  (C) myself  (D) mine

**Solution:** After a preposition (*between*), use the object case.
**Answer: (B) me**

## Part B: Timed Set (20 questions, 15 minutes)

Questions are arranged from easy to hard. The answer key is at the end.

**Q1.** Each of the players ___ given a medal.
(A) were  (B) are  (C) was  (D) have been

**Q2.** The number of applicants ___ increased this year.
(A) have  (B) has  (C) are  (D) were

**Q3.** By the time we reached the station, the train ___.
(A) left  (B) had left  (C) has left  (D) leaves

**Q4.** I ___ this movie twice already.
(A) saw  (B) have seen  (C) had seen  (D) see

**Q5.** She ___ dinner when the phone rang.
(A) cooks  (B) cooked  (C) was cooking  (D) has cooked

**Q6.** He is ___ European student.
(A) a  (B) an  (C) the  (D) no article

**Q7.** ___ sun rises in the east.
(A) A  (B) An  (C) The  (D) No article

**Q8.** She is very good ___ mathematics.
(A) in  (B) at  (C) on  (D) with

**Q9.** The book is divided ___ ten chapters.
(A) in  (B) by  (C) into  (D) with

**Q10.** Neither the manager nor the employees ___ aware of the change.
(A) was  (B) were  (C) is  (D) has been

**Q11.** Choose the correct sentence.
(A) He don't know the answer.
(B) He doesn't knows the answer.
(C) He doesn't know the answer.
(D) He didn't knew the answer.

**Q12.** The prize was shared between Rahul and ___.
(A) I  (B) me  (C) myself  (D) mine

**Q13.** Which sentence is grammatically correct?
(A) Walking down the road, a dog bit me.
(B) While I was walking down the road, a dog bit me.
(C) Walking down the road, a dog was biting me.
(D) A dog, walking down the road, bit me.

**Q14.** In the sentence *"She spoke very softly,"* the word **softly** is a/an:
(A) adjective  (B) noun  (C) adverb  (D) preposition

**Q15.** If I ___ you, I would apologise.
(A) am  (B) was  (C) were  (D) be

**Q16.** Many a student ___ failed this exam.
(A) have  (B) has  (C) are  (D) were

**Q17.** The furniture in the rooms ___ new.
(A) are  (B) were  (C) is  (D) have been

**Q18.** I have been waiting ___ two hours.
(A) since  (B) for  (C) from  (D) at

**Q19.** He said that he ___ the work the previous day.
(A) finished  (B) has finished  (C) had finished  (D) finishes

**Q20.** Choose the correct sentence.
(A) One of my friends are coming tonight.
(B) One of my friends is coming tonight.
(C) One of my friend is coming tonight.
(D) One of my friends were coming tonight.

## ✅ Answer Key (check only after attempting)

| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|:-:|:-:|---|
| 1 | **C** | *Each* is singular, past tense → *was*. |
| 2 | **B** | *The number of* is singular → *has*. |
| 3 | **B** | Two past events, the train left earlier → past perfect. |
| 4 | **B** | *Already* signals present perfect. |
| 5 | **C** | Action in progress when another past action interrupted → past continuous. |
| 6 | **A** | *European* starts with a "yoo" consonant sound. |
| 7 | **C** | The sun is unique → *the*. |
| 8 | **B** | *Good at*. |
| 9 | **C** | *Divided into*. |
| 10 | **B** | With *neither…nor*, the verb agrees with the nearer subject (*employees*, plural). |
| 11 | **C** | After *doesn't*, the verb stays in base form. |
| 12 | **B** | Object case after the preposition *between*. |
| 13 | **B** | In A, C and D, the dog appears to be the one walking. B names who is walking. |
| 14 | **C** | *Softly* describes the verb *spoke* → adverb. |
| 15 | **C** | Unreal condition uses *were* for all persons. |
| 16 | **B** | *Many a* + singular noun takes a singular verb. |
| 17 | **C** | *Furniture* is uncountable and singular. |
| 18 | **B** | *For* is used with a duration. |
| 19 | **C** | Reported speech: earlier time shifts to past perfect. |
| 20 | **B** | *One of* + plural noun, but the verb is singular. |
