# 🔥 Common Vocabulary Mistakes

## 1. Memorizing Only the Definition

❌

```text
concise = brief
```

Better:

```text
concise
→ brief
→ concise explanation
→ concise answer
→ concise report
```

Learn the word **in usage**.

---

## 2. Choosing a Related Word Instead of a Synonym

Example:

```text
rapid
```

Options may contain:

```text
fast       ← synonym
energetic  ← related
strong     ← related
active     ← related
```

Only one may actually preserve the meaning.

---

## 3. Ignoring Context

A word can have multiple meanings.

Example:

```text
address
```

```text
address a problem → deal with
address of a house → location
address an audience → speak to
```

Always check context.

---

## 4. Ignoring Part of Speech

Example:

> He completed the task ______.

Options:

```text
success
successful
successfully
succeed
```

Correct:

```text
successfully
```

---

## 5. Guessing Idioms Literally

❌

> break the ice = physically break ice

✅

> break the ice = start a friendly interaction

---

## 6. Confusing Similar Words

Maintain a separate list:

```text
affect / effect
advice / advise
accept / except
principal / principle
compliment / complement
stationary / stationery
```

# ⚡ 30-Second Vocabulary Checklist

Before answering:

```text
☐ Do I know the exact meaning?
☐ What part of speech is required?
☐ Is it a synonym or antonym?
☐ What meaning does the context require?
☐ Is the word positive or negative?
☐ Is there a root/prefix clue?
☐ Can I eliminate options?
☐ Does the option fit naturally?
```

# 🏆 Golden Rule

> **Don't collect words. Build usable vocabulary.**

For every important word, know:

```text
MEANING
+
SYNONYM
+
ANTONYM
+
PART OF SPEECH
+
USAGE
```

That turns vocabulary from memorization into a skill.
