# 1. What Is Vocabulary?

**Vocabulary** is the collection of words a person knows and understands, including their meanings and correct usage.

For placement exams, vocabulary is not just memorizing definitions.

You should know:

```text
word
 ↓
meaning
 ↓
synonym
 ↓
antonym
 ↓
part of speech
 ↓
usage
 ↓
collocation
```

### Example

**Reliable**

```text
Meaning → can be trusted
Synonym → dependable
Antonym → unreliable
Part of speech → adjective
Usage → a reliable employee
```

# 3. Synonyms

A **synonym** is a word with the same or nearly the same meaning.

### Examples

| Word      | Synonym      |
| --------- | ------------ |
| abundant  | plentiful    |
| accurate  | correct      |
| brave     | courageous   |
| concise   | brief        |
| difficult | challenging  |
| eager     | enthusiastic |
| enormous  | huge         |
| reliable  | dependable   |
| rapid     | fast         |
| genuine   | authentic    |

### ⚠️ Important

Synonyms are not always interchangeable.

Example:

> **big** and **large**

Usually interchangeable.

But:

> **strong** and **powerful**

may have different natural usages.

Always check the sentence.

# 4. Antonyms

An **antonym** expresses an opposite meaning.

### Examples

| Word        | Antonym     |
| ----------- | ----------- |
| abundant    | scarce      |
| ancient     | modern      |
| artificial  | natural     |
| expand      | contract    |
| flexible    | rigid       |
| generous    | stingy      |
| optimistic  | pessimistic |
| permanent   | temporary   |
| transparent | opaque      |
| victory     | defeat      |

### Three Common Types

#### 1. Gradable

```text
hot ↔ cold
big ↔ small
easy ↔ difficult
```

There can be intermediate levels.

#### 2. Complementary

```text
alive ↔ dead
true ↔ false
present ↔ absent
```

Usually mutually exclusive.

#### 3. Relational

```text
buy ↔ sell
give ↔ receive
teacher ↔ student
```

The relationship reverses.

# 5. Contextual Vocabulary

This is one of the most important skills.

A word can have multiple meanings.

### Example

**Address**

```text
address a problem → deal with a problem

address → location

address an audience → speak to an audience
```

So don't memorize only:

> address = location ❌

Instead learn:

> **meaning depends on context** ✅

### Context Method

```text
Read sentence
     ↓
Understand situation
     ↓
Identify meaning
     ↓
Replace with option
     ↓
Check whether meaning remains unchanged
```

# 12. Commonly Confused Words

## Affect vs Effect

**Affect** → usually verb

> The weather affected the match.

**Effect** → usually noun

> The medicine had a positive effect.

---

## Advice vs Advise

**Advice** → noun

> He gave me good advice.

**Advise** → verb

> I advise you to practice.

---

## Accept vs Except

**Accept** → receive/agrees

> She accepted the offer.

**Except** → excluding

> Everyone came except Rahul.

---

## Principal vs Principle

**Principal** → main/head of school

**Principle** → rule/belief

---

## Compliment vs Complement

**Compliment** → praise

**Complement** → completes/improves something

---

## Its vs It's

```text
its → possession
it's → it is / it has
```

---

## Then vs Than

```text
then → time/sequence
than → comparison
```


### Memory Tricks

**Necessary**

> One **c**, two **s**

**Accommodation**

> Double **c**, double **m**

**Separate**

> There is **a** in separate.

**Definitely**

> It contains **finite**, not "definately".

# 15. Part of Speech

Vocabulary questions can test the correct form of a word.

### Example

```text
success      → noun
successful   → adjective
successfully → adverb
succeed      → verb
```

### Example Question

> She completed the task ______.

The blank needs an adverb.

✅ **successfully**

Not:

❌ success
❌ successful
❌ succeed

# 16. Vocabulary Elimination

When you don't know the exact word:

### Step 1 — Check part of speech

If the question requires a noun, eliminate verbs/adjectives.

### Step 2 — Check positive/negative meaning

Example:

> The manager praised his ______ attitude.

Likely positive.

Eliminate negative options.

### Step 3 — Check intensity

```text
good
excellent
outstanding
```

These are not always interchangeable.

### Step 4 — Check context

Use the sentence to determine the exact sense.

### Step 5 — Substitute

Put the option back into the sentence.

# 18. Top 10 Exam Questions

### Q1. What is a synonym?

A word with the same or nearly the same meaning.

### Q2. What is an antonym?

A word with an opposite meaning.

### Q3. Should you learn synonyms in isolation?

No. Learn them with context and usage.

### Q4. What should you do when a word has multiple meanings?

Use the surrounding sentence to determine the intended meaning.

### Q5. What is an idiom?

A fixed expression whose overall meaning is not necessarily literal.

### Q6. What is a phrasal verb?

A verb combined with a particle/preposition producing a particular meaning.

### Q7. Why learn roots?

One root can help you understand many unfamiliar words.

### Q8. What is the best way to remember vocabulary?

```text
word
+ meaning
+ synonym
+ antonym
+ sentence
+ revision
```

### Q9. What should you do if you don't know a word?

Use:

```text
context
part of speech
prefix/root
positive/negative tone
elimination
```

### Q10. What is the biggest vocabulary mistake?

Memorizing definitions without learning how the word is actually used.

# 19. 30-Second Revision

```text
VOCABULARY
    ↓
SYNONYM / ANTONYM
    ↓
CONTEXT
    ↓
IDIOMS
    ↓
PHRASAL VERBS
    ↓
ONE-WORD SUBSTITUTION
    ↓
ROOTS + PREFIXES
    ↓
WORD FAMILIES
    ↓
CONFUSING WORDS
    ↓
SPELLING
```

### 🧠 Golden Rule

> **Don't learn 1,000 random words. Learn fewer high-frequency words deeply and repeatedly.**

A practical placement strategy is to focus on a high-frequency core, learn words in context, and connect them through roots and word families rather than relying only on rote lists.
