# Part A — Representative Questions

## Q1. Subject–Verb Agreement

Find the error:

> The list of selected candidates / are displayed / on the notice board / today.

**Answer:** `are`

### Correction

> The list of selected candidates **is displayed** on the notice board today.

**Rule:** The subject is **list**.

---

## Q2. Tense

Find the error:

> When we reached the station, the train has already left.

**Answer:** `has`

### Correction

> When we reached the station, the train **had already left**.

**Rule:** Earlier past action → past perfect.

---

## Q3. Preposition

Find the error:

> She is very good in mathematics.

**Answer:** `in`

### Correction

> She is very good **at** mathematics.

**Rule:** `good at`

---

## Q4. Parallelism

Choose the correct sentence.

A. He likes reading, writing and to travel.
B. He likes reading, writing and travelling.
C. He likes read, writing and travel.
D. He likes to reading, writing and travel.

**Answer:** **B**

---

## Q5. Comparison

Choose the correct sentence.

A. This solution is more better than that one.
B. This solution is better than that one.
C. This solution is most better than that one.
D. This solution is more best than that one.

**Answer:** **B**
