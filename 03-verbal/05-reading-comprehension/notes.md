# 1. What Is Reading Comprehension?

**Reading Comprehension (RC)** is the ability to understand, interpret, and analyze a passage and answer questions based only on the information provided.

A passage may test:

```text
understanding
interpretation
inference
vocabulary
tone
main idea
author's purpose
```

Placement-oriented RC commonly tests direct information, inference, vocabulary in context, and main idea/tone.

---

# 2. What RC Tests

## 🔹 1. Direct Understanding

Can you find information explicitly stated?

---

## 🔹 2. Main Idea

Can you identify what the **whole passage** is mainly about?

---

## 🔹 3. Inference

Can you logically conclude something that is **not directly stated**?

---

## 🔹 4. Vocabulary in Context

Can you determine what a word means **in that particular sentence**?

---

## 🔹 5. Tone

Can you identify the author's attitude?

Examples:

```text
neutral
optimistic
critical
cautious
analytical
supportive
skeptical
concerned
```

---

## 🔹 6. Purpose

Why did the author write the passage?

Examples:

```text
to explain
to inform
to criticize
to compare
to persuade
to describe
to discuss
to highlight
```

# 3. The RC Solving Method

Use this process:

```text
QUESTION SCAN
     ↓
READ PASSAGE
     ↓
IDENTIFY TOPIC
     ↓
UNDERSTAND STRUCTURE
     ↓
LOCATE EVIDENCE
     ↓
ELIMINATE OPTIONS
     ↓
ANSWER
```

### Step 1 — Scan the questions

Look at the question stems before reading the options.

Identify:

```text
main idea?
detail?
inference?
vocabulary?
tone?
purpose?
```

This gives your reading a purpose.

---

### Step 2 — Read the passage actively

Don't try to memorize every sentence.

Ask:

> "What is this paragraph doing?"

---

### Step 3 — Identify the topic

The **topic** is the subject being discussed.

Example:

> A passage discusses how remote work affects employee productivity.

Topic:

✅ **Remote work and productivity**

---

### Step 4 — Find the central idea

Ask:

> "What is the author saying about this topic?"

Topic:

> Remote work

Main idea:

> Remote work can improve productivity when organizations provide appropriate communication and management systems.

---

### Step 5 — Locate evidence

For specific questions, return to the relevant sentence or paragraph.

---

### Step 6 — Eliminate

Remove options that are:

```text
too broad
too narrow
unsupported
contradictory
extreme
partially correct
```

# 5. Main Idea Questions

### Common Question Stems

```text
What is the main idea of the passage?

What is the passage primarily about?

Which statement best summarizes the passage?

What is the central theme?
```

---

## How to Solve

Ask:

> **What idea connects all paragraphs?**

The correct answer should cover the **whole passage**.

### Example

Suppose a passage discusses:

```text
Paragraph 1 → rise of electric vehicles
Paragraph 2 → environmental benefits
Paragraph 3 → charging challenges
Paragraph 4 → future development
```

Main idea:

✅ **The development of electric vehicles, including their benefits and challenges.**

Not:

❌ Charging infrastructure

That is only one part.

---

# 6. Direct Detail Questions

The answer is explicitly present in the passage.

### Common Stems

```text
According to the passage...

The passage states that...

Which of the following is mentioned?

According to the author...
```

### Strategy

```text
Question
 ↓
Keyword
 ↓
Find location in passage
 ↓
Read surrounding lines
 ↓
Answer
```

Don't depend on memory if the passage is available.

---

# 7. Inference Questions

An **inference** is a conclusion logically supported by the passage even though it may not be directly stated.

### Common Stems

```text
It can be inferred that...

The passage suggests that...

The author implies that...

Which of the following can be concluded?
```

---

## Golden Rule

> **Inference must come from the passage, not from your general knowledge.**

This is one of the biggest RC traps.

---

### Example

Passage:

> The company introduced flexible working hours. Employee satisfaction increased, and absenteeism decreased during the following six months.

Inference:

✅ Flexible working hours may contribute to employee satisfaction.

But:

❌ Flexible working hours always increase productivity.

The second statement goes beyond the evidence.

---

# 8. Vocabulary-in-Context

The question asks what a word means **as used in the passage**.

### Example

> The company's growth began to **accelerate** after the new strategy was introduced.

Accelerate means:

A. stop
B. slow down
C. increase in speed
D. disappear

✅ **increase in speed**

---

## Important

Don't automatically choose the dictionary meaning.

Read:

```text
sentence containing word
+
previous sentence
+
following sentence
```

Then substitute the option.

# 9. Tone Questions

**Tone** = the author's attitude toward the subject.

### Common Tones

| Tone        | Meaning                |
| ----------- | ---------------------- |
| Neutral     | no strong emotion      |
| Objective   | fact-based             |
| Analytical  | examines logically     |
| Optimistic  | positive outlook       |
| Critical    | points out problems    |
| Cautious    | avoids certainty       |
| Skeptical   | doubts something       |
| Concerned   | highlights risks       |
| Supportive  | favors/defends an idea |
| Persuasive  | tries to convince      |
| Informative | mainly explains        |
| Sarcastic   | uses mockery           |

---

## Tone Clues

Look at the author's word choice.

### Positive

```text
beneficial
successful
promising
effective
remarkable
```

Possible tone:

```text
optimistic
supportive
positive
```

### Negative

```text
failure
problem
danger
flawed
harmful
```

Possible tone:

```text
critical
concerned
skeptical
```

### Neutral

```text
data
research
study
according to
analysis
```

Possible tone:

```text
objective
analytical
informative
```

# 10. Purpose Questions

Purpose asks:

> **Why did the author write this passage?**

### Common purposes

```text
to explain
to inform
to describe
to compare
to criticize
to persuade
to highlight
to examine
to discuss
```

### Example

If a passage explains how blockchain works:

Purpose:

✅ **To explain blockchain technology.**

Not:

❌ To prove blockchain is the best technology.

That introduces an unsupported judgment.

---

# 11. Title Questions

The best title should be:

```text
specific enough
+
broad enough
+
connected to the entire passage
```

### Avoid

❌ Too broad

> Technology

❌ Too narrow

> One Charging Problem in Electric Vehicles

Better:

✅ **The Challenges and Future of Electric Vehicles**

---

# 12. Author's View

Sometimes the question asks what the author believes.

### Look for:

```text
however
but
although
clearly
unfortunately
surprisingly
may
might
likely
perhaps
```

These words reveal qualification or attitude.

---

## Strong vs Cautious Language

### Strong

```text
always
never
certainly
undoubtedly
completely
```

### Cautious

```text
may
might
could
possibly
often
generally
appears to
```

### ⚠️ Trap

If the passage says:

> This method **may** improve efficiency.

Don't choose:

> This method **will definitely** improve efficiency.

The second is stronger than the evidence.

# 14. Elimination Technique

When two options look correct, compare them carefully.

## Eliminate Option 1 — Too Broad

Passage:

> Discusses benefits and limitations of online education.

Option:

❌ Online education has changed the world.

Too broad.

---

## Eliminate Option 2 — Too Narrow

Option:

❌ Online education requires internet access.

True but too narrow.

---

## Eliminate Option 3 — Outside Information

Option:

❌ Online education is cheaper in every country.

If the passage doesn't establish this:

❌ Eliminate.

---

## Eliminate Option 4 — Extreme

Watch for:

```text
always
never
all
none
completely
only
must
definitely
```

Unless the passage clearly supports such language.

---

# 15. Common Traps

## Trap 1 — True but Not the Answer

An option can be factually mentioned but still not answer the question.

---

## Trap 2 — Outside Knowledge

Your real-world knowledge doesn't matter if the passage doesn't support it.

---

## Trap 3 — Extreme Statements

Be careful with:

```text
always
never
everyone
no one
completely
only
```

---

## Trap 4 — Half-Correct Option

Example:

Passage says:

> A can improve productivity but may increase communication difficulties.

Option:

> A improves productivity and eliminates communication problems.

❌ First part correct, second part wrong.

---

## Trap 5 — Main Idea vs Detail

```text
MAIN IDEA → entire passage
DETAIL → one specific part
```

---

## Trap 6 — Inference vs Guess

```text
Inference → logically supported
Guess → merely possible
```

---

# 16. Time Management

For a short placement passage:

```text
Question scan → 15–20 sec
Passage → 2–3 min
Questions → 30–60 sec each
```

Don't spend excessive time rereading the entire passage.

For a detail question:

```text
Question
 ↓
Keyword
 ↓
Locate
 ↓
Answer
```

For an inference question:

```text
Question
 ↓
Relevant evidence
 ↓
Logical connection
 ↓
Answer
```

# 17. Worked Passage

### Passage

> Many companies have adopted remote work because digital communication tools make collaboration easier across locations. Remote work can reduce commuting time and provide employees with greater flexibility. However, organizations may face challenges in maintaining team coordination and communication. As a result, companies increasingly use structured meetings, collaboration platforms, and clear communication policies to maintain productivity.

---

### Q1. What is the main idea?

A. Remote work is always better than office work.
B. Remote work offers benefits but requires effective communication systems.
C. Digital tools are replacing offices completely.
D. Employees dislike commuting.

✅ **Answer: B**

Why?

It covers:

```text
benefits
+
challenges
+
organizational response
```

---

### Q2. According to the passage, what is one benefit of remote work?

A. Eliminating meetings
B. Reducing commuting time
C. Removing communication problems
D. Increasing office space

✅ **Answer: B**

Directly stated.

---

### Q3. What can be inferred?

A. Remote work has no disadvantages.
B. Communication remains important in remote work environments.
C. Companies should eliminate offices.
D. Employees always prefer remote work.

✅ **Answer: B**

It follows logically from the passage.

---

### Q4. What is the tone?

A. Angry
B. Sarcastic
C. Balanced/analytical
D. Humorous

✅ **Answer: C**

The passage discusses both benefits and challenges objectively.

---

### Q5. Why do companies use structured meetings?

A. To increase commuting
B. To replace employees
C. To maintain coordination and productivity
D. To eliminate digital tools

✅ **Answer: C**

---

# 18. Top 10 Exam Questions

### Q1. What is the main idea?

The central message covering the entire passage.

### Q2. What is an inference?

A conclusion logically supported by the passage.

### Q3. Should outside knowledge be used?

No. Answer according to the passage.

### Q4. What is tone?

The author's attitude toward the subject.

### Q5. What is purpose?

The reason the author wrote the passage.

### Q6. How do you solve vocabulary questions?

Use the surrounding context.

### Q7. What is the biggest main-idea trap?

Choosing a true but overly narrow detail.

### Q8. What is the biggest inference trap?

Choosing an option that is possible but not supported.

### Q9. How do you solve direct questions?

Find the relevant keyword and verify it in the passage.

### Q10. What makes a good title?

It should represent the central idea without being too broad or too narrow.
