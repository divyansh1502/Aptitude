# 📖 Passage 1 — Technology

> Digital learning has changed the way students access educational resources. Online platforms allow learners to study from different locations and often provide flexible schedules. However, access to technology alone does not guarantee effective learning. Students also need suitable study habits, reliable resources, and guidance from teachers or mentors. Therefore, successful digital learning depends not only on technology but also on how learners and institutions use it.

### Q1. What is the main idea?

A. Online education should replace traditional education.
B. Technology is the only factor responsible for successful learning.
C. Effective digital learning depends on both technology and how it is used.
D. Students cannot learn without teachers.

### Q2. What is one benefit of digital learning mentioned in the passage?

A. It eliminates examinations.
B. It provides flexible schedules.
C. It removes the need for study habits.
D. It guarantees effective learning.

### Q3. What can be inferred?

A. Technology by itself may not guarantee learning success.
B. Online learning is always better.
C. Teachers are unnecessary in digital learning.
D. Students dislike online platforms.

### Q4. What is the tone of the passage?

A. Angry
B. Balanced and analytical
C. Humorous
D. Sarcastic

### Q5. What does "access" mean in the first sentence?

A. Ability to obtain/use
B. Removal
C. Restriction
D. Failure

# 📖 Passage 2 — Exercise

> Regular physical activity has several benefits for overall well-being. Exercise can improve cardiovascular fitness, strengthen muscles, and support mental health. Nevertheless, exercise should be approached sensibly. Excessive training without adequate recovery may lead to fatigue or injury. A balanced routine that combines activity with sufficient rest is therefore more sustainable than constantly increasing workout intensity.

### Q6. What is the central idea?

A. More exercise is always better.
B. Exercise has benefits, but a balanced approach is important.
C. Exercise should be avoided because it causes injury.
D. Only cardiovascular exercise is useful.

### Q7. According to the passage, excessive training may cause:

A. improved recovery
B. greater flexibility
C. fatigue or injury
D. better sleep only

### Q8. What does "sustainable" mean in the passage?

A. difficult to maintain
B. able to continue over time
C. extremely intense
D. immediately effective

### Q9. Which statement is supported?

A. Rest has no role in training.
B. Increasing intensity constantly is always beneficial.
C. Recovery should be part of a balanced routine.
D. Exercise only benefits physical health.

### Q10. The author is primarily:

A. encouraging a balanced approach
B. criticizing all forms of exercise
C. promoting extreme training
D. discussing professional athletes only

# 📖 Passage 3 — Artificial Intelligence

> Artificial intelligence is increasingly being used in workplaces to automate repetitive tasks and assist employees with data analysis. This can allow workers to focus on tasks requiring judgment, creativity, and communication. However, the adoption of AI also creates a need for employees to develop new skills. Organizations that introduce technology without providing opportunities for learning may struggle to realize its full benefits. Thus, technological adoption and employee development need to progress together.

### Q11. What is the main idea?

A. AI will eliminate all human jobs.
B. AI is useful, but organizations must also invest in employee development.
C. Employees should avoid learning new skills.
D. AI can only perform creative tasks.

### Q12. What is one benefit of AI mentioned?

A. Eliminating communication
B. Automating repetitive tasks
C. Removing employee training
D. Preventing data analysis

### Q13. What can be inferred?

A. Technology adoption may be less effective without employee training.
B. AI cannot analyze data.
C. Employees do not need judgment.
D. Organizations should stop adopting AI.

### Q14. The word "adoption" most nearly means:

A. rejection
B. introduction/use
C. destruction
D. restriction

### Q15. What is the author's tone?

A. Balanced
B. Hostile
C. Sarcastic
D. Fearful

However, public transportation systems need to be reliable, accessible, and convenient if people are expected to use them regularly. Simply expanding a transport network without considering service quality may not produce the intended results.

### Q16. What is the passage mainly about?

D. Traffic congestion has no solution.

### Q17. What condition does the author emphasize?

A. Public transport must be expensive.
B. Public transport should be reliable and convenient.
C. People should stop travelling.
D. Roads should be removed.

### Q18. What can be inferred?

# 🧠 Passage 5 — Advanced

> Many organizations collect large amounts of data, but possessing data does not automatically lead to better decisions. Data must be accurate, relevant, and interpreted appropriately. Poor-quality data can produce misleading conclusions, while excessive data without a clear objective can make analysis more difficult. Effective decision-making therefore requires not simply more information, but useful information combined with sound judgment.

### Q19. What is the main idea?

### Q20. What problem can poor-quality data cause?

### Q21. What can be inferred?

### Q22. What does "relevant" mean in context?
