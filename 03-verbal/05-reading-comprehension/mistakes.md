# 🔥 Common RC Mistakes

## 1. Choosing a Detail as the Main Idea

### Mistake

You select something mentioned in one paragraph.

### Fix

Ask:

> Does this answer cover the entire passage?

If not, it's probably a supporting detail.

---

# 2. Using Outside Knowledge

### Mistake

> "But in real life this is true..."

That doesn't matter.

### Fix

```text
PASSAGE = YOUR WORLD
```

Only use information supported by the passage.

---

# 3. Making an Extreme Inference

Passage:

> The method **may** improve efficiency.

Wrong inference:

> The method **always** improves efficiency.

### Fix

Watch for:

```text
always
never
all
none
only
definitely
completely
```

---

# 4. Choosing a Plausible Inference

An option may sound logical but still not be supported.

### Ask:

> **Can I point to evidence in the passage that supports this?**

If not:

❌ Don't choose it.

---

# 5. Ignoring "NOT / EXCEPT"

Example:

> Which of the following is **NOT** mentioned?

Your brain may automatically search for a correct statement.

### Fix

Circle mentally:

```text
NOT
EXCEPT
INCORRECT
FALSE
```

---

# 6. Vocabulary Without Context

### Mistake

Choosing the dictionary meaning.

### Fix

Read:

```text
sentence before
+
target sentence
+
sentence after
```

Then substitute the option.

---

# 7. Rereading the Whole Passage

### Mistake

Going back to the beginning for every question.

### Fix

For direct questions:

```text
question keyword
↓
locate paragraph
↓
read surrounding lines
```

---

# 8. Confusing Tone With Topic

Topic:

> AI in education

Tone:

> cautious

Don't answer a tone question with the subject of the passage.

---

# 9. Choosing Extreme Tone

Passage may be mildly critical.

Options:

```text
critical
angry
hostile
furious
```

Correct may be:

✅ critical

Not:

❌ furious

---

# 10. Selecting a Too-Broad Title

Passage:

> Benefits and challenges of remote work.

Bad title:

❌ Modern Society

Better:

✅ Remote Work: Benefits and Challenges

# 🔁 Weekly RC Review

At the end of every week:

### Step 1

Find your most frequent mistake.

### Step 2

Solve 5 questions of only that type.

Example:

```text
Inference mistakes
↓
5 inference questions
↓
Analyze every wrong answer
```

### Step 3

Return to mixed RC.

# ⚡ RC Final Checklist

Before selecting an answer:

```text
☐ What type of question is this?
☐ Where is the evidence?
☐ Am I answering from the passage?
☐ Is this option too broad?
☐ Is it too narrow?
☐ Is it extreme?
☐ Does it contradict the passage?
☐ Is it only partially correct?
☐ For inference, can I prove it?
☐ For tone, does the whole passage support it?
```

# 🏆 Golden Rule

```text
MAIN IDEA
→ WHOLE PASSAGE

DETAIL
→ EXPLICIT LINE

INFERENCE
→ LOGICAL EVIDENCE

VOCABULARY
→ CONTEXT

TONE
→ AUTHOR'S ATTITUDE

PURPOSE
→ AUTHOR'S REASON

TITLE
→ CENTRAL IDEA
```

> **Don't answer what you think is true. Answer what the passage supports.**
