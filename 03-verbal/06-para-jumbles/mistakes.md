# 🔴 Common Mistakes

## 1. Choosing a Pronoun Sentence as Opener — `G`

### Mistake

Starting with:

```text
This...
It...
They...
These...
```

without checking what the word refers to.

### Fix

Ask:

> "What does this pronoun refer to?"

If the answer is another sentence, that sentence probably comes first.

---

# 2. Ignoring Mandatory Pairs — `C`

### Mistake

Trying to arrange all five sentences simultaneously.

### Fix

First search for:

```text
noun → pronoun
A/an → the
cause → effect
question → answer
claim → example
```

---

# 3. Misreading Connectors — `R`

### Mistake

Seeing:

```text
However
```

and placing it randomly.

### Fix

Ask:

> "What idea is this sentence contrasting with?"

---

# 4. Treating "Therefore" as Automatically Last — `C`

### Mistake

```text
Therefore = final sentence
```

### Fix

Remember:

```text
Therefore = result
```

A result can still be followed by an explanation or consequence.

---

# 5. Ignoring Chronology — `R`

### Mistake

Putting later events before earlier events.

### Fix

Circle time markers:

```text
first
then
later
afterward
finally
previously
```

---

# 6. Reading for "Story Feel" Only — `C`

### Mistake

Choosing the sequence that simply sounds natural.

### Fix

Use objective links:

```text
Pronoun
Connector
Chronology
Cause-effect
Reference
```

---

# 7. Overthinking After Finding the Correct Chain — `T`

### Mistake

Spending another 2–3 minutes after finding a strong sequence.

### Fix

Use:

```text
Opener
+
Mandatory Pair
+
Flow Check
=
Answer
```

## Day 1

Learn:

```text

Chronology
```

Solve 10 questions.

---

## Day 2

Review mistakes.

Solve 10 new questions.

Focus only on your weakest clue.

```text
10 questions

```

```text
Day 1 accuracy
```

# 🏆 Golden Rule

> **Don't memorize the sequence. Understand why each sentence follows the previous one.**

```text
OPENER
   ↓
REFERENCE
   ↓
MANDATORY PAIR
   ↓
LOGICAL CONNECTION
   ↓
CONCLUSION
```
