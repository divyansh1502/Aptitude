## Q1. Percentage Change

**Type:** PATTERN

A price is increased by 20% and then decreased by 10%. Find the net change.

### Solution

```text
Net change
= 20 - 10 - (20 × 10)/100
= 10 - 2
= 8%
```

**Answer:** 8% increase

**Concept:** Successive percentage

---

## Q2. Average

**Type:** PATTERN

The average of 10 numbers is 25. If one number 40 is replaced by 50, find the new average.

```text
Old Total = 10 × 25
          = 250

New Total = 250 - 40 + 50
          = 260

New Average = 260 / 10
            = 26
```

**Answer:** 26

---

## Q3. Time & Work

**Type:** PATTERN

A can complete a job in 12 days and B in 18 days. Find the time taken when they work together.

```text
A = 1/12

B = 1/18

Together:

1/12 + 1/18
= 5/36
```

Therefore:

```text
Time = 36/5
     = 7.2 days
```

**Answer:** 7.2 days

---

## Q4. Profit & Loss

**Type:** PATTERN

An article is purchased for ₹800 and sold for ₹920. Find the profit percentage.

```text
Profit = 920 - 800
       = ₹120

Profit % = (120 / 800) × 100
         = 15%
```

**Answer:** 15%

---

## Q5. Ratio

**Type:** PATTERN

The ratio of A:B is 3:5. If their total is 64, find A and B.

```text
3x + 5x = 64

8x = 64

x = 8
```

Therefore:

```text
A = 24
B = 40
```

**Answer:** 24 and 40

---

## Q6. HCF / LCM

**Type:** PATTERN

Find the LCM of 12, 18 and 30.

```text
12 = 2² × 3
18 = 2 × 3²
30 = 2 × 3 × 5

LCM = 2² × 3² × 5
    = 180
```

**Answer:** 180

---

## Q7. Time-Speed-Distance

**Type:** PATTERN

A car travels 120 km at 60 km/h. Find the time taken.

```text
Time = Distance / Speed

     = 120 / 60

     = 2 hours
```

**Answer:** 2 hours

---

## Q8. Probability

**Type:** PATTERN

A bag contains 5 red and 3 blue balls. One ball is selected randomly. Find the probability of selecting a blue ball.

```text
Total = 5 + 3 = 8

Favourable = 3

Probability = 3/8
```

**Answer:** 3/8

```text
×2
```

## Q35. Pronoun

**Type:** PATTERN

Incorrect:

> Every student must bring their ID card.

> Every student must bring **his or her** ID card.

# 13. Common Traps

## Quantitative

### Trap 1 — Successive Percentages

Do not simply add percentage changes.

Wrong:

```text
+20% - 10% = +10%
```

Correct:

```text
20 - 10 - 2 = 8%
```

---

### Trap 2 — Average

Remember:

```text
Total = Average × Number
```

---

## Reasoning

### Trap 3 — Assuming Unstated Information

Use only the conditions provided.

---

### Trap 4 — Direction

Draw the path if there are multiple turns.

---

## Pseudocode

### Trap 5 — Integer Division

```text
17 / 5 = 3
```

when both operands are integers.

---

### Trap 6 — Modulus

```text
17 % 5 = 2
```

not 3.

---

### Trap 7 — Indexing

Most programming languages used in these assessments use:

```text
index 0 → first element
```

---

## Verbal

### Trap 8 — Grammar by Sound

A sentence sounding natural does not guarantee grammatical correctness.

Check:

```text
Subject
Verb
Tense
Agreement
Preposition
```

---

## Coding

### Trap 9 — Complexity

Before implementing, ask:

```text
Can I sort?
Can I hash?
Can I use two pointers?
Can I use prefix sum?
Can I use greedy?
Can I use DP?
```
