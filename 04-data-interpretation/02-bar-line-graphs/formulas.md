# 1. Basic Formulas

### Total

$$
Total=A+B+C+\cdots
$$

### Difference

$$
Difference=|A-B|
$$

### Average

$$
Average=\frac{Sum}{Number}
$$

---

# 2. Percentage

$$
Percentage=\frac{Part}{Whole}\times100
$$

### Part

$$
Part=\frac{Percentage\times Whole}{100}
$$

### Whole

$$
Whole=\frac{Part\times100}{Percentage}
$$

---

# 3. Percentage Change

### Percentage Increase

$$
\frac{New-Old}{Old}\times100
$$

### Percentage Decrease

$$
\frac{Old-New}{Old}\times100
$$

### Absolute Change

$$
New-Old
$$

---

# 4. Average

$$
Average=\frac{x_1+x_2+\cdots+x_n}{n}
$$

### Total from Average

$$
Total=Average\times n
$$

---

# 5. Ratio

$$
Ratio=A:B
$$

Simplify using HCF.

Example:

$$
300:450=2:3
$$

---

# 6. Graph Scale

If:

$$
1\ graph\ unit=k\ actual\ units
$$

and the graph value is `x`:

$$
Actual\ Value=x\times k
$$

### Example

1 graph unit = 50 students.

Bar = 8 units.

$$
8\times50=400
$$

Actual value = **400 students**.

---

# 7. Growth

### Absolute Growth

$$
Growth=New-Old
$$

### Percentage Growth

$$
Growth\%=\frac{New-Old}{Old}\times100
$$

### Growth Factor

$$
Growth\ Factor=\frac{New}{Old}
$$

---

# 8. Successive Percentage Change

For changes `a%` and `b%`:

$$
Net\ Change=a+b+\frac{ab}{100}
$$

Use signs:

* Increase → `+`
* Decrease → `−`

Example:

Increase 20%, then decrease 10%:

$$
20-10-\frac{20\times10}{100}
$$

$$
=8\%
$$

Net increase = **8%**

# 10. Calculation Shortcuts

## Percentage Multipliers

| Change | Multiplier |
| -----: | ---------: |
|   +10% |       1.10 |
|   +20% |       1.20 |
|   +25% |       1.25 |
|   +50% |       1.50 |
|   −10% |       0.90 |
|   −20% |       0.80 |
|   −25% |       0.75 |
|   −50% |       0.50 |

---

## Cancel Before Multiplication

$$
\frac{250}{1000}\times100
$$

$$
=\frac14\times100
$$

$$
=25\%
$$
