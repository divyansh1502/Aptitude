# 1. C — Calculation Mistakes

Examples:

* Wrong addition
* Wrong subtraction
* Wrong multiplication
* Wrong division
* Percentage calculation error
* Decimal error
* Ratio simplification error

### Prevention

* Calculate step-by-step.
* Cancel before multiplying.
* Estimate the expected answer.
* Recheck calculations when options are close.

---

# 2. S — Selection Mistakes

Selection mistakes happen when the wrong graph value is selected.

Examples:

* Reading 2023 instead of 2024
* Selecting Product B instead of Product C
* Using revenue instead of profit
* Comparing the wrong two categories

### Prevention

Write:

```text id="i0ep1k"
Required:
Category:
Year:
Metric:
Value 1:
Value 2:
```

before calculating difficult questions.

---

# 3. T — Graph Reading Mistakes

This is one of the most important categories for graph DI.

Common mistakes:

* Ignoring the scale
* Misreading the axis
* Ignoring the legend
* Confusing X-axis and Y-axis
* Missing units
* Reading a nearby bar/point
* Assuming visual height equals exact numerical value

### Prevention

Always check:

```text id="rj9xwy"
Title
↓
X-axis
↓
Y-axis
↓
Scale
↓
Unit
↓
Legend
```

---

# 4. R — Reasoning Mistakes

Examples:

* Using the wrong percentage denominator
* Confusing absolute growth with percentage growth
* Reversing a ratio
* Using simple average incorrectly
* Confusing percentage points with percentage change

### Example

Old = 400

New = 500

Wrong:

$$
\frac{100}{500}\times100=20\%
$$

Correct:

$$
\frac{100}{400}\times100=25\%
$$

---

# 5. G — Guess / Time Management Mistakes

Examples:

* Spending too long reading a graph
* Performing unnecessary calculations
* Guessing despite having enough time
* Rushing the final calculation
* Not moving to an easier question

### Prevention

Use:

```text id="j4y8qm"
Read
↓
Identify
↓
Calculate
↓
Verify
```

If a question becomes unnecessarily time-consuming, move on when the test format permits.

# 🚨 Most Common Graph DI Traps

## Trap 1 — Ignoring Scale

If:

```text id="z2u9x0"
1 unit = 50
```

and graph reading = 8:

$$
8\times50=400
$$

Not 8.

---

## Trap 2 — Confusing Absolute and Percentage Increase

Old = 200

New = 300

Absolute increase:

$$
100
$$

Percentage increase:

$$
\frac{100}{200}\times100=50\%
$$

---

## Trap 3 — Wrong Bar in Multiple Graphs

Always identify the legend before reading a bar.

---

## Trap 4 — Wrong Year

For a line graph, carefully identify the exact year requested.

---

## Trap 5 — Wrong Percentage Denominator

Percentage change uses the original value.

---

## Trap 6 — Visual Estimation

Two bars may look similar but represent significantly different values.

Read the scale.
