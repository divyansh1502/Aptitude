# 1. Basic Arithmetic

### Total

$$
Total=A+B+C+\cdots
$$

### Difference

$$
Difference=|A-B|
$$

### Average

$$
Average=\frac{Sum}{Number\ of\ values}
$$

---

# 2. Percentage

### Basic Percentage

$$
Percentage=\frac{Part}{Whole}\times100
$$

### Part

$$
Part=\frac{Percentage\times Whole}{100}
$$

### Whole

$$
Whole=\frac{Part\times100}{Percentage}
$$

---

# 3. Percentage Increase

$$
Percentage\ Increase=
\frac{New-Old}{Old}\times100
$$

### Increase

$$
Increase=New-Old
$$

### New Value

$$
New=Old\left(1+\frac{x}{100}\right)
$$

---

# 4. Percentage Decrease

$$
Percentage\ Decrease=
\frac{Old-New}{Old}\times100
$$

### Decrease

$$
Decrease=Old-New
$$

### New Value

$$
New=Old\left(1-\frac{x}{100}\right)
$$

---

# 5. Ratio

### Basic Ratio

$$
A:B
$$

### Simplified Ratio

Divide both terms by their HCF.

Example:

$$
240:360
$$

$$
=2:3
$$

### If Ratio is Given

If:

$$
A:B=2:3
$$

Then:

$$
A=2x,\quad B=3x
$$

---

# 6. Average

### Simple Average

$$
Average=\frac{x_1+x_2+\cdots+x_n}{n}
$$

### Total from Average

$$
Total=Average\times Number
$$

### Number of Values

$$
Number=\frac{Total}{Average}
$$

---

# 7. Difference

### Absolute Difference

$$
|A-B|
$$

### A is x more than B

$$
A-B=x
$$

### A is x less than B

$$
B-A=x
$$

---

# 8. Percentage of Total

If a category contributes `A` and total is `T`:

$$
Percentage=\frac{A}{T}\times100
$$

### Example

A = 250

Total = 1000

$$
\frac{250}{1000}\times100=25\%
$$

# 10. Reverse Percentage

If a value becomes `N` after an increase of `x%`:

$$
Original=\frac{N}{1+\frac{x}{100}}
$$

If a value becomes `N` after a decrease of `x%`:

$$
Original=\frac{N}{1-\frac{x}{100}}
$$

### Example

After a 25% increase, value = 500.

$$
Original=\frac{500}{1.25}=400
$$

---

# 11. Weighted Average

If values have different weights:

$$
Weighted\ Average=
\frac{\sum wx}{\sum w}
$$

where:

* `x` = value
* `w` = weight

### Example

10 students → average 80

30 students → average 60

$$
Average=
\frac{10(80)+30(60)}{10+30}
$$

$$
=65
$$

---

# 12. Successive Percentage Change

For two successive changes of `a%` and `b%`:

$$
Net\ Change=
a+b+\frac{ab}{100}
$$

Use the signs:

* Increase → positive
* Decrease → negative

### Example

Increase by 20%, then increase by 10%:

$$
20+10+\frac{20\times10}{100}
$$

$$
=32\%
$$

Net increase = **32%**

---

### Increase 20%, Decrease 10%

$$
20-10-\frac{20\times10}{100}
$$

$$
=8\%
$$

Net increase = **8%**

# 14. Ratio-Proportion Shortcuts

### Ratio to Fraction

If:

$$
A:B=2:3
$$

Then total parts:

$$
2+3=5
$$

A's share:

$$
\frac{2}{5}
$$

B's share:

$$
\frac{3}{5}
$$

---

### Ratio to Percentage

For:

$$
A:B=2:3
$$

A percentage:

$$
\frac{2}{5}\times100=40\%
$$

B percentage:

$$
\frac{3}{5}\times100=60\%
$$

# 15. DI Calculation Shortcuts

## Shortcut 1 — Cancel Before Multiplication

$$
\frac{250}{1000}\times100
$$

Cancel:

$$
\frac{1}{4}\times100=25\%
$$

---

## Shortcut 2 — Use Known Fractions

Instead of calculating:

$$
\frac{125}{500}\times100
$$

Recognize:

$$
125:500=1:4
$$

Therefore:

$$
25\%
$$

---

## Shortcut 3 — Use Multipliers

20% increase on 500:

$$
500\times1.20=600
$$

20% decrease on 500:

$$
500\times0.80=400
$$

---

## Shortcut 4 — Estimate

When answer options are far apart, approximation can save time.

# 16. Quick Cheat Sheet

```text
Percentage
= Part / Whole × 100

Percentage Increase
= (New − Old) / Old × 100

Percentage Decrease
= (Old − New) / Old × 100

Average
= Sum / Number

Total
= Average × Number

Difference
= |A − B|

Ratio
= A : B

New after x% increase
= Old × (1 + x/100)

New after x% decrease
= Old × (1 − x/100)

Original after x% increase
= New / (1 + x/100)

Original after x% decrease
= New / (1 − x/100)

Weighted Average
= Σwx / Σw

Successive Change
= a + b + ab/100
```

### 🧠 Golden Rule

> **For percentage change, the original value is the denominator.**
