# 1. C — Calculation Mistakes

Common errors:

* Wrong percentage calculation
* Addition/subtraction error
* Ratio simplification error
* Wrong multiplication
* Decimal mistake
* Arithmetic error

### Prevention

Write the formula first.

Then calculate.

---

# 2. S — Selection Mistakes

Examples:

* Using the wrong number from the paragraph
* Using the wrong year
* Selecting the wrong category
* Using total instead of subgroup total

### Prevention

Write:

```text id="k14i2w"
Required:
Base:
Given:
Formula:
```

before solving multi-step questions.

---

# 3. T — Interpretation Mistakes

This is the most important category for caselets.

Common errors:

* Missing the word "remaining"
* Missing the word "of"
* Applying a percentage to the wrong group
* Confusing "increase by" with "increase to"
* Ignoring units
* Misreading the relationship between two statements

### Example

> 20% of technical employees are managers.

Correct:

$$
Managers=20\%\times Technical
$$

Not:

$$
20\%\times Total
$$

---

# 4. R — Reasoning Mistakes

Common errors:

* Incorrect ratio parts
* Wrong denominator for percentage change
* Incorrect sequence of calculations
* Treating successive percentages as simple addition
* Comparing percentages instead of actual values

---

# 5. G — Guess / Time Management Mistakes

Common problems:

* Re-reading the paragraph repeatedly
* Not creating a mini-table
* Calculating every value even when unnecessary
* Spending too long on one question
* Guessing because the paragraph looks complicated

### Prevention

Use:

```text id="l3p5rc"
Extract
↓
Organize
↓
Question
↓
Calculate
↓
Verify
```

# 🚨 Most Common Caselet Traps

## Trap 1 — Wrong Base

> 30% of A are B.

The base is **A**, not the total.

---

## Trap 2 — Remaining

If:

A = 40%

B = 25%

C = 15%

Then:

$$
Remaining=20\%
$$

---

## Trap 3 — Ratio Parts

For:

$$
3:2
$$

Total parts:

$$
5
$$

---

## Trap 4 — Successive Percentages

Increase by 20%, then decrease by 20%.

It does **not** return to the original value.

$$
20-20-\frac{20\times20}{100}
$$

$$
=-4\%
$$

Net decrease = **4%**.

---

## Trap 5 — Different Bases

Example:

> 40% of total employees are technical.
> 25% of technical employees are testers.

Tester percentage of total:

$$
40\%\times25\%=10\%
$$

# 🎯 Final Rule

> **Never fight the paragraph directly. Convert it into a small table first.**

### Caselet Master Formula

```text
Paragraph
    ↓
Extract Numbers
    ↓
Find the Base
    ↓
Build Mini-Table
    ↓
Apply Formula
    ↓
Verify
```

**Caselet DI = Reading + Organization + Calculation.**
