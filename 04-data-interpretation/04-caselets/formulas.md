# 1. Percentage

### Part from Percentage

$$
Part=\frac{P}{100}\times Total
$$

### Percentage

$$
P=\frac{Part}{Total}\times100
$$

### Total

$$
Total=\frac{Part\times100}{P}
$$

---

# 2. Remaining Values

### Remaining Percentage

$$
Remaining\%=100-\sum Known\%
$$

### Remaining Value

$$
Remaining=Total-\sum Known\ Values
$$

---

# 3. Ratio

For:

$$
A:B=a:b
$$

Total ratio parts:

$$
a+b
$$

One part:

$$
\frac{Total}{a+b}
$$

A:

$$
\frac{a}{a+b}\times Total
$$

B:

$$
\frac{b}{a+b}\times Total
$$

---

# 4. Average

$$
Average=\frac{Sum}{n}
$$

### Total

$$
Sum=Average\times n
$$

### Missing Value

$$
Missing=Total\ Sum-Known\ Sum
$$

---

# 5. Percentage Change

### Increase

$$
\frac{New-Old}{Old}\times100
$$

### Decrease

$$
\frac{Old-New}{Old}\times100
$$

---

# 6. Weighted Average

If values have weights:

$$
Weighted\ Average=
\frac{\sum(Value\times Weight)}
{\sum Weight}
$$

### Example

Group A:

100 people with average 60

Group B:

200 people with average 70

$$
Average=
\frac{100(60)+200(70)}
{300}
$$

$$
=66.67
$$

---

# 7. Successive Percentage

For two successive changes:

$$
Net=a+b+\frac{ab}{100}
$$

Use negative signs for decreases.

Example:

+20%, −10%:

$$
20-10-\frac{200}{100}=8\%
$$

# 9. Caselet Shortcuts

## Ratio Shortcut

For:

$$
3:2
$$

total parts:

$$
5
$$

So:

* First = 3/5 of total
* Second = 2/5 of total

---

## Percentage Shortcut

If:

$$
25\%=\frac14
$$

then simply divide the total by 4.

If:

$$
20\%=\frac15
$$

divide by 5.

---

## Multi-Level Percentage

If:

> A = 40% of total
> B = 25% of A

Then:

$$
B=40\%\times25\%\times Total
$$

$$
=10\%\times Total
$$

---

## Average Shortcut

If average is known:

$$
Total=Average\times Number
$$

Then use the total to find missing values.

# 10. Quick Cheat Sheet

```text id="y1zz9v"
Part
= Percentage × Total / 100

Percentage
= Part / Total × 100

Total
= Part × 100 / Percentage

Remaining %
= 100 − Known %

Remaining Value
= Total − Known Values

Ratio a:b
→ Total Parts = a + b

One Part
= Total / (a+b)

A
= a/(a+b) × Total

Average
= Sum / n

Total
= Average × n

Missing Value
= Total − Known Values

% Increase
= (New − Old) / Old × 100

% Decrease
= (Old − New) / Old × 100

Weighted Average
= Σ(Value × Weight) / ΣWeight
```

### 🧠 Caselet Formula Rule

> **First identify the base. Then apply the percentage.**

This single habit prevents many caselet mistakes.
