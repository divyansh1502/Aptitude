# 📝 Data Interpretation — Caselet Practice

> Placement-focused caselet practice covering percentages, ratios, averages, multi-step calculations, and comparisons.

## 📌 Instructions

* Target time: **35 minutes**
* First extract the data.
* Build a mini-table for long caselets.
* Do not calculate unnecessary values.
* Target accuracy: **90%+**

---

# 🟢 Caselet 1 — Company Employees

A company has **1200 employees**. 40% work in development, 25% work in testing, 15% work in HR, and the remaining employees work in administration.

### Q1. How many employees work in development?

### Q2. How many employees work in testing?

### Q3. How many employees work in HR?

### Q4. How many employees work in administration?

### Q5. Find the ratio of development employees to administration employees.

---

# 🟡 Caselet 2 — College Students

A college has **2000 students**. 35% are from CSE, 25% are from ECE, 20% are from Mechanical, and the remaining students belong to Civil and other branches.

### Q6. Find the number of CSE students.

### Q7. Find the number of ECE students.

### Q8. Find the number of Mechanical students.

### Q9. Find the percentage of students from Civil and other branches combined.

### Q10. Find the ratio of CSE to ECE students.

---

# 🟠 Caselet 3 — Sales

A company records sales of **500, 600, 700, 800 and 900 units** over five months.

### Q11. Find the total sales.

### Q12. Find the average monthly sales.

### Q13. Find the difference between the highest and lowest monthly sales.

### Q14. What percentage of total sales occurred in the fifth month?

### Q15. Find the ratio of first-month sales to fifth-month sales.

---

# 🔵 Caselet 4 — Ratio

A school has **1500 students**. The ratio of boys to girls is **3:2**.

Among the boys, 40% participate in sports. Among the girls, 30% participate in sports.

### Q16. Find the number of boys.

### Q17. Find the number of girls.

### Q18. Find the number of boys participating in sports.

### Q19. Find the number of girls participating in sports.

### Q20. Find the total number of students participating in sports.

# 🔴 Caselet 5 — Multi-Step

A company has **2400 employees**. 50% are technical employees. Of the technical employees, 60% are developers, 25% are testers, and the remaining are technical support staff.

### Q21. Find the number of technical employees.

### Q22. Find the number of developers.

### Q23. Find the number of testers.

### Q24. Find the number of technical support employees.

### Q25. Find the ratio of developers to testers.

---

# 🟣 Caselet 6 — Advanced

A company's revenue increased from **₹40 lakh to ₹50 lakh** in one year. In the following year, revenue increased by another 20%.

### Q26. Find the percentage increase in the first year.

### Q27. Find the revenue in the second year.

### Q28. Find the revenue after the second 20% increase.

### Q29. Find the total percentage increase from the original ₹40 lakh.

### Q30. Find the absolute increase from the original revenue to the final revenue.
