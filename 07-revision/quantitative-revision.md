# PAGE 1 — Arithmetic Core

## 01. Number System

| Concept | Formula / Rule |
|---|---|
| Even number | Divisible by 2 |
| Divisibility by 3 | Sum of digits divisible by 3 |
| Divisibility by 4 | Last 2 digits divisible by 4 |
| Divisibility by 5 | Last digit 0 or 5 |
| Divisibility by 6 | Divisible by 2 and 3 |
| Divisibility by 8 | Last 3 digits divisible by 8 |
| Divisibility by 9 | Sum of digits divisible by 9 |
| Divisibility by 11 | Difference of alternating digit sums divisible by 11 |
| Sum 1 to n | `n(n+1)/2` |
| Sum squares | `n(n+1)(2n+1)/6` |
| Sum cubes | `[n(n+1)/2]²` |
| Arithmetic mean | `Sum / Number of terms` |

## 02. HCF & LCM

For two positive integers:

\[
\boxed{\text{HCF}\times\text{LCM}=a\times b}
\]

For fractions:

\[
HCF=\frac{HCF\text{ of numerators}}{LCM\text{ of denominators}}
\]

\[
LCM=\frac{LCM\text{ of numerators}}{HCF\text{ of denominators}}
\]

Use prime factorization for quick HCF/LCM.

---

## 03. Percentages

\[
\%=\frac{Part}{Whole}\times100
\]

\[
x\%\text{ of }N=\frac{xN}{100}
\]

\[
\%\text{ change}=\frac{New-Original}{Original}\times100
\]

Increase by `r%`:

\[
N(1+r/100)
\]

Decrease by `r%`:

\[
N(1-r/100)
\]

Successive changes:

\[
a+b+\frac{ab}{100}
\]

Use increase `+`, decrease `−`.

Common:

`50%=1/2`, `25%=1/4`, `20%=1/5`, `10%=1/10`, `12.5%=1/8`, `5%=1/20`.

## 04. Ratio & Proportion

\[
a:b=\frac ab
\]

If:

\[
a:b=c:d
\]

then:

\[
ad=bc
\]

Divide total `T` in ratio `a:b`:

\[
\frac{aT}{a+b},\quad\frac{bT}{a+b}
\]

Direct proportion:

\[
x\propto y
\]

Inverse proportion:

\[
x\propto\frac1y
\]

## 05. Averages

\[
Average=\frac{Sum}{Number}
\]

\[
Sum=Average\times Number
\]

Combined average:

\[
\frac{n_1a_1+n_2a_2}{n_1+n_2}
\]

If one value `x` is replaced by `y`:

\[
New\ Average=Old\ Average+\frac{y-x}{n}
\]

## 06. Profit & Loss

\[
Profit=SP-CP
\]

\[
Loss=CP-SP
\]

\[
Profit\%=\frac{Profit}{CP}\times100
\]

\[
Loss\%=\frac{Loss}{CP}\times100
\]

\[
SP=CP(1+Profit\%/100)
\]

\[
SP=CP(1-Loss\%/100)
\]

Discount:

\[
Discount=MP-SP
\]

\[
Discount\%=\frac{Discount}{MP}\times100
\]

## 07. Simple & Compound Interest

Simple Interest:

\[
SI=\frac{PRT}{100}
\]

\[
Amount=P+SI
\]

Compound Amount:

\[
A=P(1+r/100)^n
\]

\[
CI=A-P
\]

For 2 years:

\[
CI=P\left[\frac{2r}{100}+\frac{r^2}{10000}\right]
\]

For half-yearly compounding:

\[
r\rightarrow r/2,\quad n\rightarrow2n
\]

## 08. Time & Work

If A completes work in `x` days:

\[
A's\ 1-day\ work=\frac1x
\]

A and B together:

\[
\frac1x+\frac1y
\]

If efficiency ratio is:

\[
A:B=m:n
\]

then time ratio is:

\[
A:B=n:m
\]

For work:

\[
Work=Efficiency\times Time
\]

## 09. Time, Speed & Distance

\[
Speed=\frac{Distance}{Time}
\]

\[
Distance=Speed\times Time
\]

\[
Time=\frac{Distance}{Speed}
\]

Conversions:

\[
1\,m/s=\frac{18}{5}\,km/h
\]

\[
1\,km/h=\frac{5}{18}\,m/s
\]

Average speed for equal distances:

\[
\boxed{\frac{2xy}{x+y}}
\]

Relative speed:

- Same direction → difference
- Opposite directions → sum

a^3-b^3=(a-b)(a^2+ab+b^2)

\[

## 11. Mixtures & Alligation

Weighted average:

\[
Average=\frac{\sum quantity\times value}{\sum quantity}
\]

Alligation:

\[
\boxed{
\frac{Quantity\ of\ cheaper}{Quantity\ of\ dearer}
=
\frac{Dearer-Mean}{Mean-Cheaper}
}
\]

Use when mixing two quantities with different prices/concentrations.

## 12. Ages

Age difference remains constant.

If present ages are in ratio `a:b` and total age is `T`:

\[
A=\frac{aT}{a+b}
\]

\[
B=\frac{bT}{a+b}
\]

For age after `n` years:

\[
Current+n
\]

For age `n` years ago:

\[
Current-n
\]

## 13. Pipes & Cisterns

If a pipe fills a tank in `x` hours:

\[
Rate=\frac1x
\]

If a pipe empties in `y` hours:

\[
Rate=-\frac1y
\]

Combined rate:

\[
\frac1x+\frac1y-\frac1z...
\]

Time:

\[
\frac1{Combined\ Rate}
\]

## 14. Boats & Streams

Let:

- Boat speed in still water = `b`
- Stream speed = `s`

Downstream:

\[
b+s
\]

Upstream:

\[
b-s
\]

Therefore:

\[
b=\frac{Downstream+Upstream}{2}
\]

\[
s=\frac{Downstream-Upstream}{2}
\]

## 15. Permutation & Combination

Permutation:

\[
^nP_r=\frac{n!}{(n-r)!}
\]

Combination:

\[
^nC_r=\frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}
\]

Important:

\[
^nC_r=^nC_{n-r}
\]

\[
^nP_r=^nC_r\times r!
\]

Circular arrangement:

\[
(n-1)!
\]

when rotations are considered identical.

## 16. Probability

\[
P(E)=\frac{Favourable\ Outcomes}{Total\ Outcomes}
\]

\[
0\le P(E)\le1
\]

Complement:

\[
P(\bar E)=1-P(E)
\]

For mutually exclusive events:

\[
P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)
\]

General addition:

\[
P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B)
\]

Independent events:

\[
P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B)
\]

## 18. Progressions

### AP

nth term:

\[
a_n=a+(n-1)d
\]

Sum:

\[
S_n=\frac n2[2a+(n-1)d]
\]

Also:

\[
S_n=\frac n2(a+l)
\]

### GP

nth term:

\[
a_n=ar^{n-1}
\]

Finite sum:

\[
S_n=a\frac{r^n-1}{r-1}
\]

for `r ≠ 1`.

Infinite GP, when:

\[
|r|<1
\]

\[
S_\infty=\frac{a}{1-r}
\]

# Exam Memory Triggers

**Percentage:** Part ÷ Whole × 100

**Profit/Loss:** Always divide by CP.

**Discount:** Always divide by MP.

**Percentage change:** Always divide by original.

**Average:** Total ÷ Count.

**Work:** Rate × Time.

**TSD:** Distance = Speed × Time.

**Probability:** Favourable ÷ Total.

**P&C:** Order matters → Permutation; order doesn't matter → Combination.

**Inverse relation:** One ↑, other ↓ when product is constant.
