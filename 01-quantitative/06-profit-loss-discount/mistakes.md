
# Profit, Loss & Discount — Common Mistakes

## 1. Using MP for Profit Percentage

### Mistake

Calculating:

```text
Profit/MP × 100
```

### Correct

Profit percentage is calculated on **CP**:

```text
Profit% = Profit/CP × 100
```

### Fix

Remember:

```text
Profit/Loss → CP
```

---

## 2. Using CP for Discount Percentage

### Mistake

Calculating discount percentage using CP.

### Correct

Discount is based on **MP**:

```text
Discount% = Discount/MP × 100
```

### Fix

Remember:

```text
Discount → MP
```

---

## 3. Adding Successive Discounts

### Mistake

Two discounts of `20%` and `10%` are treated as `30%`.

### Correct

```text
20 + 10 - (20×10)/100
= 28%
```

### Fix

Second discount applies to the already-discounted price.

---

## 4. Confusing MP and SP

### Mistake

Assuming:

```text
MP = SP
```

when a discount is given.

### Correct

```text
MP → Discount → SP
```

---

## 5. Forgetting the Direction of Profit/Loss

### Mistake

Using:

```text
CP - SP
```

for profit.

### Correct

```text
Profit = SP - CP
Loss = CP - SP
```

### Fix

Higher selling price → profit.

Lower selling price → loss.

---

## 6. Applying Profit Percentage to SP

### Mistake

For `20%` profit:

```text
SP = CP + 20% of SP
```

### Correct

Profit is `20% of CP`.

```text
SP = CP × 120/100
```

---

## 7. Applying Discount to CP

### Mistake

Taking a discount directly from CP.

### Correct

Discount is applied to **MP**.

```text
SP = MP × (100-d)/100
```

---

## 8. Assuming Equal Profit and Loss Percentages Always Cancel

If two articles have equal CP and one has `20%` profit while another has `20%` loss, they cancel.

But if their CPs are different, they do not necessarily cancel.

### Fix

Always calculate:

```text
Total CP
Total SP
```

before finding the overall result.

---

## 9. Using the Same SP Formula for Profit and Loss

### Mistake

For `20%` loss:

```text
SP = CP × 120/100
```

### Correct

For loss:

```text
SP = CP × 80/100
```

### Fix

```text
Profit → 100 + p
Loss   → 100 - p
```

---

## 10. Forgetting Reverse Calculation

If an item is sold at `20%` profit for `₹600`, do not calculate:

```text
600 - 20%
```

Profit percentage is based on CP.

Correct:

```text
CP = 600 × 100/120
   = ₹500
```

### Fix

For reverse questions, use the multiplier/ratio method.

---

# Quick Revision Checklist

```text
□ Did I identify CP, SP and MP?
□ Is profit/loss percentage based on CP?
□ Is discount percentage based on MP?
□ Did I apply discount before comparing SP with CP?
□ Did I avoid adding successive discounts directly?
□ Did I update the base when reversing percentages?
□ For multiple articles, did I compare total CP and total SP?
□ Did I check whether CPs or SPs are equal?
□ Did I use the correct profit/loss multiplier?
□ Did I distinguish marked price from selling price?
```
