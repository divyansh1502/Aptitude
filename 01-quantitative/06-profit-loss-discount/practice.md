# Profit, Loss & Discount — Practice

## Part A — Representative Questions

### Q1. Profit Percentage

An article is bought for `₹800` and sold for `₹920`. Find the profit percentage.

### Solution

```text
Profit = 920 - 800
       = ₹120
```

Profit percentage:

```text
Profit% = 120/800 × 100
        = 15%
```

**Answer: `15%`**

---

### Q2. Find CP

An article is sold for `₹960` at a profit of `20%`. Find its cost price.

### Solution

At `20%` profit:

```text
SP = CP × 120/100
```

Therefore:

```text
CP = 960 × 100/120
   = ₹800
```

**Answer: `₹800`**

---

### Q3. Discount

A shirt has a marked price of `₹2000`. A discount of `15%` is offered. Find the selling price.

### Solution

Discount:

```text
15% of 2000
= ₹300
```

Therefore:

```text
SP = 2000 - 300
   = ₹1700
```

**Answer: `₹1700`**

---

### Q4. Profit After Discount

An article costs `₹800`. It is marked at `₹1000` and sold after a `10%` discount. Find the profit percentage.

### Solution

Selling price:

```text
SP = 1000 × 90/100
   = ₹900
```

Profit:

```text
Profit = 900 - 800
       = ₹100
```

Profit percentage:

```text
Profit% = 100/800 × 100
        = 12.5%
```

**Answer: `12.5%`**

---

### Q5. Successive Discounts

An article is marked at `₹5000`. It is offered at successive discounts of `20%` and `10%`. Find the final selling price and effective discount.

### Solution

After `20%` discount:

```text
5000 × 80/100
= ₹4000
```

After `10%` discount:

```text
4000 × 90/100
= ₹3600
```

Final selling price:

```text
₹3600
```

Total discount:

```text
5000 - 3600
= ₹1400
```

Effective discount:

```text
1400/5000 × 100
= 28%
```

**Answer: `₹3600`, effective discount = `28%`**

```text
1.25x + 200 = 1.20(x + 200)
```

```text
1.25x + 200 = 1.20x + 240
```

```text
0.05x = 40
```

```text

```
