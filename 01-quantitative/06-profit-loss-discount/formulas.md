# Profit, Loss & Discount — Formulas

## 1. Basic Formulas

```text
Profit = SP - CP
Loss = CP - SP
Discount = MP - SP
```

---

## 2. Profit Percentage

```text
Profit% = (Profit/CP) × 100
```

```text
Profit% = ((SP-CP)/CP) × 100
```

---

## 3. Loss Percentage

```text
Loss% = (Loss/CP) × 100
```

```text
Loss% = ((CP-SP)/CP) × 100
```

---

## 4. SP at Profit

For `p%` profit:

```text
SP = CP × (100+p)/100
```

---

## 5. SP at Loss

For `p%` loss:

```text
SP = CP × (100-p)/100
```

---

## 6. CP from Profit

For `p%` profit:

```text
CP = SP × 100/(100+p)
```

---

## 7. CP from Loss

For `p%` loss:

```text
CP = SP × 100/(100-p)
```

---

## 8. Discount Percentage

```text
Discount% = (Discount/MP) × 100
```

---

## 9. SP After Discount

For `d%` discount:

```text
SP = MP × (100-d)/100
```

---

## 10. MP from Discount

```text
MP = SP × 100/(100-d)
```

---

## 11. Successive Discounts

For `a%` and `b%`:

```text
Effective Discount
= a + b - ab/100
```

---

## 12. Successive Multiplicative Method

For discounts `a%`, `b%`, `c%`:

```text
Final SP
= MP × (1-a/100)
     × (1-b/100)
     × (1-c/100)
```

---

## 13. Profit After Discount

```text
SP = MP × (100-d)/100
```

Then:

```text
Profit% = (SP-CP)/CP × 100
```

---

## 14. MP Required for Desired Profit

If desired profit = `p%` and discount = `d%`:

```text
Required SP
= CP × (100+p)/100
```

```text
Required MP
= Required SP × 100/(100-d)
```

Combined:

```text
MP
= CP × (100+p)/(100-d)
```

---

## 15. MP as Percentage of CP

If MP is `p%` above CP:

```text
MP = CP × (100+p)/100
```

After `d%` discount:

```text
SP
= CP × (100+p)/100 × (100-d)/100
```

---

## 16. Overall Profit/Loss

For multiple articles:

```text
Total Profit = Total SP - Total CP
```

```text
Total Loss = Total CP - Total SP
```

Percentage:

```text
Overall Profit%
= Total Profit/Total CP × 100
```

```text
Overall Loss%
= Total Loss/Total CP × 100
```

---

## 17. Same SP, Equal Profit/Loss

If two articles are sold at the same SP, one at `x%` profit and another at `x%` loss:

```text
Overall Loss%
= x²/100
```

---

## 18. Same CP

If two articles have the same CP:

```text
Equal profit% + Equal loss%
→ No overall profit/loss
```

---

## 19. False Weight

If `w` units are given while charging for `100` units:

```text
Gain%
= (100-w)/w × 100
```

---

## 20. Percentage Multipliers

```text
p% profit  → × (1+p/100)

p% loss    → × (1-p/100)

d% discount → × (1-d/100)
```

---

## 21. Ratio Method

At `p%` profit:

```text
CP : SP = 100 : (100+p)
```

At `p%` loss:

```text
CP : SP = 100 : (100-p)
```

At `d%` discount:

```text
MP : SP = 100 : (100-d)
```

---

## 22. Quick Table

| Situation                | Formula               |
| ------------------------ | --------------------- |
| Profit                   | `SP - CP`             |
| Loss                     | `CP - SP`             |
| Discount                 | `MP - SP`             |
| Profit %                 | `Profit/CP × 100`     |
| Loss %                   | `Loss/CP × 100`       |
| Discount %               | `Discount/MP × 100`   |
| SP at profit             | `CP(100+p)/100`       |
| SP at loss               | `CP(100-p)/100`       |
| CP from profit           | `SP×100/(100+p)`      |
| CP from loss             | `SP×100/(100-p)`      |
| SP after discount        | `MP(100-d)/100`       |
| MP from discount         | `SP×100/(100-d)`      |
| Two successive discounts | `a+b-ab/100`          |
| Same SP, ±x%             | `x²/100` overall loss |
