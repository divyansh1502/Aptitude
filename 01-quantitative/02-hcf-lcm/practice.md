# HCF & LCM — Practice

## Part A — Representative Questions

### Q1. Direct HCF

Find the HCF of `84` and `126`.

**Solution**

Prime factorize:

```text
84 = 2² × 3 × 7
126 = 2 × 3² × 7
```

Take minimum powers:

```text
HCF = 2 × 3 × 7
    = 42
```

**Answer: `42`**

---

### Q2. Direct LCM

Find the LCM of `18`, `24`, and `30`.

**Solution**

Prime factorize:

```text
18 = 2 × 3²
24 = 2³ × 3
30 = 2 × 3 × 5
```

Take maximum powers:

```text
LCM = 2³ × 3² × 5
    = 8 × 9 × 5
    = 360
```

**Answer: `360`**

---

### Q3. Euclidean Algorithm

Find the HCF of `867` and `255`.

**Solution**

```text
867 = 255 × 3 + 102

255 = 102 × 2 + 51

102 = 51 × 2 + 0
```

Last non-zero remainder:

```text
51
```

**Answer: `51`**

---

### Q4. Greatest Number with Same Remainder

Find the greatest number that divides `75`, `105`, and `135`, leaving the same remainder.

**Solution**

Find the differences:

```text
105 - 75 = 30
135 - 105 = 30
135 - 75 = 60
```

Now:

```text
HCF(30,30,60) = 30
```

**Answer: `30`**

---

### Q5. Bells

Three bells ring every `12`, `18`, and `24` minutes. If they ring together now, after how many minutes will they ring together again?

**Solution**

Prime factorization:

```text
12 = 2² × 3
18 = 2 × 3²
24 = 2³ × 3
```

LCM:

```text
2³ × 3²
= 8 × 9
= 72
```

**Answer: `72 minutes`**
