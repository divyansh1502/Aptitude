# Mixtures — Practice

## 🟢 Part A — Representative Questions

### Q1. Basic Concentration

A mixture contains 20 L milk and 10 L water. Find the percentage of milk.

**Solution**

Total mixture:

```text
20 + 10 = 30 L
```

Milk percentage:

```text
20/30 × 100
= 66.67%
```

**Answer: 66⅔%**

---

### Q2. Equal Quantity Alligation

A 30% solution and a 50% solution are mixed to obtain a 40% solution. Find the ratio.

**Solution**

Using alligation:

```text
30% : 50%
= (50 - 40) : (40 - 30)
= 10 : 10
= 1 : 1
```

**Answer: 1 : 1**

---

### Q3. Price Mixture

Rice costing ₹40/kg and ₹60/kg is mixed to obtain rice costing ₹48/kg. Find the ratio.

**Solution**

```text
Cheaper : Dearer
= (60 - 48) : (48 - 40)
= 12 : 8
= 3 : 2
```

**Answer: 3 : 2**

---

### Q4. Mixing Solutions

20 L of 30% acid is mixed with 10 L of 60% acid. Find the final concentration.

**Solution**

Acid in first solution:

```text
20 × 30/100 = 6 L
```

Acid in second solution:

```text
10 × 60/100 = 6 L
```

Total acid:

```text
6 + 6 = 12 L
```

Total mixture:

```text
20 + 10 = 30 L
```

Final concentration:

```text
12/30 × 100
= 40%
```

**Answer: 40%**

---

### Q5. Replacement

A vessel contains 40 L milk. 10 L is removed and replaced with water. Find the percentage of milk left.

**Solution**

Milk removed:

```text
10 L
```

Milk remaining:

```text
40 - 10 = 30 L
```

Total mixture remains:

```text
40 L
```

Milk percentage:

```text
30/40 × 100
= 75%
```

**Answer: 75%**
