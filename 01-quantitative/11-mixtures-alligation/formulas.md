# Mixtures — Formulas

## 📌 Basic Formulas

| Concept          | Formula                       |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Concentration %  | `Solute / Mixture × 100`      |
| Solute quantity  | `Mixture × Concentration/100` |
| Solvent quantity | `Mixture - Solute`            |
| Mixture quantity | `Solute + Solvent`            |
| Average price    | `Total Cost / Total Quantity` |

---

## ⚖️ Weighted Average

```text
Weighted Average
= (q₁v₁ + q₂v₂) / (q₁ + q₂)
```

For multiple quantities:

```text
Average
= Σ(qv) / Σq
```

### Use when

* Quantities are unequal.
* Prices/concentrations are different.
* You need the final average value.

---

## 🔀 Alligation

If:

```text
Lower = L
Higher = H
Mean = M
```

Then:

```text
Lower : Higher
= (H - M) : (M - L)
```

### Memory

```text
Quantity of cheaper
= Difference between dearer and mean

Quantity of dearer
= Difference between mean and cheaper
```

---

## 🧪 Mixing Solutions

```text
Final concentration
= Total solute / Total mixture × 100
```

For two solutions:

```text
Final %
= (q₁p₁ + q₂p₂)/(q₁ + q₂)
```

---

## 💧 Dilution

When only solvent is added:

```text
Solute remains constant
```

Therefore:

```text
Initial Solute = Final Solute
```

Useful equation:

```text
V₁C₁ = V₂C₂
```

where concentration can be represented as a decimal or percentage consistently.

### Water added

```text
Water added = Final volume - Initial volume
```

---

## 🔄 Replacement

If:

```text
Total quantity = V
Quantity removed = x
```

Fraction remaining:

```text
(V - x)/V
```

or:

```text
1 - x/V
```

---

## 🔁 Repeated Replacement

After `n` operations:

```text
Original substance remaining
= Initial quantity × (1 - x/V)ⁿ
```

### Original substance removed

```text
Initial quantity
- Remaining quantity
```

---

## 🥛 Milk and Water

If:

```text
Milk : Water = a : b
Total = T
```

Then:

```text
Milk = T × a/(a+b)
```

```text
Water = T × b/(a+b)
```

### Milk percentage

```text
Milk %
= a/(a+b) × 100
```

---

## 💰 Mixture Cost

```text
Total Cost
= q₁p₁ + q₂p₂
```

```text
Cost per unit
= Total Cost / Total Quantity
```

---

## 📈 Profit on Mixture

```text
Profit = SP - CP
```

```text
Profit %
= Profit/CP × 100
```

```text
SP
= CP × (100 + Profit%)/100
```

---

## 📉 Loss on Mixture

```text
Loss = CP - SP
```

```text
Loss %
= Loss/CP × 100
```

```text
SP
= CP × (100 - Loss%)/100
```

---

## 🧮 Equation Method

For two mixtures:

```text
q₁p₁ + q₂p₂
= Final Quantity × Final %
```

For percentages, either use:

```text
30%, 50%, 40%
```

consistently, or convert all to decimals:

```text
0.30, 0.50, 0.40
```

---

## 🎯 Quick Shortcuts

### Equal quantities

If equal quantities of `A` and `B` are mixed:

```text
Average = (A + B)/2
```

### Equal concentrations

```text
Final concentration = same concentration
```

### Add pure solvent

```text
Solute unchanged
Concentration decreases
```

### Add pure solute

```text
Solute increases
Concentration increases
```

### Remove mixture

Removed quantity has the **current concentration**.

---

## 🧠 Mental Rules

```text
Mean must lie between the two values.

Closer value to the mean → larger quantity required.

Farther value from the mean → smaller quantity required.
```

Example:

```text
40 and 80 → target 45
```

45 is close to 40, so:

```text
40-value quantity > 80-value quantity
```

---

## 📋 When to Use What?

| Situation                      | Method                        |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------------- |
| Find concentration             | `Solute/Total × 100`          |
| Find solute                    | `Total × %`                   |
| Unequal quantities + average   | Weighted average              |
| Two values + desired mean      | Alligation                    |
| Add water/solvent              | Solute constant               |
| Remove and replace once        | Direct calculation            |
| Remove and replace repeatedly  | Replacement formula           |
| Complicated unknown quantities | Equation method               |
| Price mixture                  | Weighted average / Alligation |
| Milk-water ratio               | Ratio                         |
| Adulteration                   | Weighted average / Alligation |
