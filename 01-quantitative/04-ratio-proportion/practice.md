# Ratio & Proportion — Practice

## Part A — Representative Questions

### Q1. Ratio with Total

The ratio of boys to girls in a class is `3 : 5`. If there are `64` students, find the number of boys and girls.

**Solution**

Total ratio parts:

```text
3 + 5 = 8
```

One part:

```text
64/8 = 8
```

Boys:

```text
3 × 8 = 24
```

Girls:

```text
5 × 8 = 40
```

**Answer: `24 boys, 40 girls`**

---

### Q2. Ratio with Difference

The ratio of two numbers is `4 : 7`. Their difference is `27`. Find the numbers.

**Solution**

Difference in ratio:

```text
7 - 4 = 3
```

Therefore:

```text
3 parts = 27
```

One part:

```text
27/3 = 9
```

Numbers:

```text
4 × 9 = 36

7 × 9 = 63
```

**Answer: `36 and 63`**

---

### Q3. Proportion

Find `x`:

```text
5 : 8 = 15 : x
```

**Solution**

Cross multiply:

```text
5x = 8 × 15

5x = 120

x = 24
```

**Answer: `24`**

---

### Q4. Direct Proportion

If `8` books cost `₹640`, what will `15` books cost?

**Solution**

Cost is directly proportional to number of books.

```text
8/640 = 15/x
```

Cross multiply:

```text
8x = 640 × 15

x = 1200
```

**Answer: `₹1200`**

---

### Q5. Inverse Proportion

`12` workers can complete a job in `15` days. How many days will `20` workers take, assuming all workers work at the same rate?

**Solution**

Workers and days are inversely proportional.

```text
Workers × Days = Constant
```

Therefore:

```text
12 × 15 = 20 × x
```

```text
180 = 20x

x = 9
```

**Answer: `9 days`**
