# Simple Interest & Compound Interest — Practice

## Part A — Representative Questions

### Q1. Simple Interest

Find the simple interest on `₹6000` at `8%` per annum for `3 years`.

### Solution

```text
SI = PRT/100
   = 6000 × 8 × 3 / 100
   = ₹1440
```

Amount:

```text
A = 6000 + 1440
  = ₹7440
```

**Answer: SI = `₹1440`**

---

### Q2. Find Principal

The simple interest on a sum at `10%` per annum for `4 years` is `₹1200`. Find the principal.

### Solution

```text
P = SI × 100/(R×T)
```

```text
P = 1200 × 100/(10×4)
  = ₹3000
```

**Answer: `₹3000`**

---

### Q3. Compound Interest

Find the compound interest on `₹10,000` at `10%` per annum for `2 years`, compounded annually.

### Solution

```text
A = P(1+R/100)^T
```

```text
A = 10000(1.10)^2
  = 10000 × 1.21
  = ₹12100
```

Therefore:

```text
CI = 12100 - 10000
   = ₹2100
```

**Answer: `₹2100`**

---

### Q4. Half-Yearly Compounding

Find the compound interest on `₹20,000` at `12%` per annum for `1 year`, compounded half-yearly.

### Solution

Half-yearly rate:

```text
12/2 = 6%
```

Number of periods:

```text
2
```

Amount:

```text
A = 20000(1.06)^2
  = 20000 × 1.1236
  = ₹22472
```

Therefore:

```text
CI = 22472 - 20000
   = ₹2472
```

**Answer: `₹2472`**

---

### Q5. Difference Between SI and CI

Find the difference between CI and SI on `₹10,000` at `10%` per annum for `2 years`.

### Solution

Shortcut:

```text
CI-SI = PR²/10000
```

Therefore:

```text
= 10000 × 10²/10000
= ₹100
```

**Answer: `₹100`**
