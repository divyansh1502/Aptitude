# Probability — Practice

## 🟢 Part A — Representative Questions

### Q1. Basic Probability

A fair die is rolled once. What is the probability of getting an even number?

**Solution**

Even outcomes:

```text
2, 4, 6
```

Favorable outcomes = `3`

Total outcomes = `6`

```text
P(E) = 3/6 = 1/2
```

**Answer: `1/2`**

---

### Q2. Complement

A fair coin is tossed 3 times. What is the probability of getting at least one Head?

**Solution**

Use complement.

Probability of no Head:

```text
P(TTT) = (1/2)^3 = 1/8
```

Therefore:

```text
P(at least one H)
= 1 - 1/8
= 7/8
```

**Answer: `7/8`**

---

### Q3. Without Replacement

A bag contains 5 red and 3 blue balls. Two balls are drawn without replacement. What is the probability that both are red?

**Solution**

First red:

```text
5/8
```

After one red is removed:

```text
4/7
```

Therefore:

```text
P(both red)
= 5/8 × 4/7
= 20/56
= 5/14
```

**Answer: `5/14`**

---

### Q4. Two Dice

Two fair dice are rolled. What is the probability that their sum is 7?

**Solution**

Total outcomes:

```text
6×6 = 36
```

Ways to obtain 7:

```text
(1,6)
(2,5)
(3,4)
(4,3)
(5,2)
(6,1)
```

Favorable outcomes = `6`

```text
P(sum 7)
= 6/36
= 1/6
```

**Answer: `1/6`**

---

### Q5. Cards

One card is drawn from a standard deck. What is the probability that it is a King or a Queen?

**Solution**

Kings = `4`

Queens = `4`

They cannot be the same card.

Favorable:

```text
4+4 = 8
```

Total:

```text
52
```

Therefore:

```text
P(King or Queen)
= 8/52
= 2/13
```

**Answer: `2/13`**
