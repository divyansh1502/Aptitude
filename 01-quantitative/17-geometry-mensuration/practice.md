# Geometry & Mensuration — Practice

## 🟢 Part A — Representative Questions

### Q1. Triangle Area

Find the area of a triangle with base `12 cm` and height `8 cm`.

**Solution**

```text
Area = 1/2 × base × height
     = 1/2 × 12 × 8
     = 48 cm²
```

**Answer: 48 cm²**

---

### Q2. Pythagoras

A right triangle has perpendicular sides `6 cm` and `8 cm`. Find its hypotenuse.

**Solution**

```text
c² = 6² + 8²
   = 36 + 64
   = 100
```

```text
c = 10 cm
```

**Answer: 10 cm**

---

### Q3. Circle

Find the area of a circle with radius `7 cm`. Use `π = 22/7`.

**Solution**

```text
Area = πr²
     = 22/7 × 7 × 7
     = 154 cm²
```

**Answer: 154 cm²**

---

### Q4. Cuboid

A cuboid has dimensions `10 cm × 6 cm × 4 cm`. Find its volume.

**Solution**

```text
Volume = lbh
       = 10×6×4
       = 240 cm³
```

**Answer: 240 cm³**

---

### Q5. Cylinder

A cylinder has radius `7 cm` and height `10 cm`. Find its volume using `π = 22/7`.

**Solution**

```text
Volume = πr²h
       = 22/7 × 7² × 10
       = 1540 cm³
```

**Answer: 1540 cm³**



### Q20


```text

```

Inner area:

```text

```

Path area:

```text
1000-756
= 244 m²
```
