# Geometry & Mensuration — Formulas

## 📐 Angles

### Complementary

```text
A + B = 90°
```

### Supplementary

```text
A + B = 180°
```

### Straight Angle

```text
180°
```

### Complete Angle

```text
360°
```

### Triangle Angle Sum

```text
A+B+C = 180°
```

### Quadrilateral Angle Sum

```text
360°
```

### Polygon Interior Angle Sum

```text
(n-2)×180°
```

### Regular Polygon Interior Angle

```text
[(n-2)×180°]/n
```

### Regular Polygon Exterior Angle

```text
360°/n
```

### Number of Polygon Diagonals

```text
n(n-3)/2
```

---

## 🔺 Triangle

### Area

```text
1/2 × base × height
```

### Perimeter

```text
a+b+c
```

### Pythagoras

```text
c² = a²+b²
```

### Equilateral Triangle

```text
Area = √3/4 × a²
```

```text
Height = √3/2 × a
```

### Heron's Formula

```text
s = (a+b+c)/2
```

```text
Area = √[s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)]
```

# 📏 Scaling

If every length is multiplied by `k`:

```text
Length → k
Area   → k²
Volume → k³
```

Examples:

```text
Side doubled:
Area   → 4 times
Volume → 8 times
```

# 🔢 Unit Conversion

### Length

```text
1 km = 1000 m
1 m = 100 cm
1 cm = 10 mm
```

### Area

```text
1 m² = 10,000 cm²
1 cm² = 100 mm²
```

### Volume

```text
1 m³ = 1,000,000 cm³
1 cm³ = 1000 mm³
```

### Capacity

```text
1 L = 1000 mL
1 mL = 1 cm³
1 L = 1000 cm³
```
