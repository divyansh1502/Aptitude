# Time & Work — Practice

## Part A — Representative Questions

### Q1. Basic Work Rate

A can complete a work in `12 days`. What fraction of the work does A complete in one day?

### Solution

```text
1-day work = 1/12
```

**Answer: `1/12`**

---

### Q2. Two People Together

A can complete a work in `10 days` and B can complete it in `15 days`. How long will they take together?

### Solution

```text
A's rate = 1/10
B's rate = 1/15
```

Together:

```text
1/10 + 1/15
= 3/30 + 2/30
= 5/30
= 1/6
```

Therefore:

```text
Time = 6 days
```

**Answer: `6 days`**

---

### Q3. Finding One Person's Time

A and B together can complete a work in `6 days`. A alone can complete it in `10 days`. How many days will B alone take?

### Solution

```text
A+B rate = 1/6
A rate = 1/10
```

Therefore:

```text
B rate = 1/6 - 1/10
       = 5/30 - 3/30
       = 2/30
       = 1/15
```

Therefore:

```text
B takes 15 days
```

**Answer: `15 days`**

---

### Q4. Men and Days

12 men can complete a work in `15 days`. How many men are required to complete the same work in `9 days`?

### Solution

For the same work:

```text
M₁D₁ = M₂D₂
```

```text
12 × 15 = M × 9
```

```text
M = 180/9
  = 20
```

**Answer: `20 men`**

---

### Q5. Efficiency

A is `50%` more efficient than B. B takes `18 days` to complete a work. How many days does A take?

### Solution

B's efficiency:

```text
100%
```

A's efficiency:

```text
150%
```

Efficiency ratio:

```text
A : B = 150 : 100
       = 3 : 2
```

Time ratio is inverse:

```text
A : B = 2 : 3
```

Therefore:

```text
A's time = 18 × 2/3
         = 12 days
```

**Answer: `12 days`**
