# Average — Practice

## Part A — Representative Questions

### Q1. Basic Average

Find the average of:

```text
18, 24, 30, 36, 42
```

**Solution**

Sum:

```text
18 + 24 + 30 + 36 + 42
= 150
```

Number of values:

```text
5
```

Average:

```text
150/5
= 30
```

**Answer: `30`**

---

### Q2. Missing Number

The average of `6` numbers is `25`. Five of the numbers are:

```text
12, 18, 24, 30, 36
```

Find the sixth number.

**Solution**

Required total:

```text
25 × 6
= 150
```

Known total:

```text
12 + 18 + 24 + 30 + 36
= 120
```

Missing number:

```text
150 - 120
= 30
```

**Answer: `30`**

---

### Q3. Replacement

The average of `10` numbers is `35`. One number `42` is replaced by `62`. Find the new average.

**Solution**

Change in total:

```text
62 - 42
= 20
```

Change in average:

```text
20/10
= 2
```

New average:

```text
35 + 2
= 37
```

**Answer: `37`**

---

### Q4. Combined Average

There are `20` students with an average score of `60`, and `30` students with an average score of `70`. Find the combined average.

**Solution**

First group total:

```text
20 × 60 = 1200
```

Second group total:

```text
30 × 70 = 2100
```

Combined total:

```text
1200 + 2100 = 3300
```

Combined students:

```text
20 + 30 = 50
```

Combined average:

```text
3300/50
= 66
```

**Answer: `66`**

---

### Q5. Average Speed

A car travels equal distances at `40 km/h` and `60 km/h`. Find its average speed.

**Solution**

For equal distances:

```text
Average Speed = 2ab/(a+b)
```

Therefore:

```text
= 2×40×60/(40+60)
= 4800/100
= 48 km/h
```

**Answer: `48 km/h`**
