## 1. Age Basics

Age problems usually involve:

* Present age
* Past age
* Future age
* Age difference
* Age ratio
* Sum of ages
* Average age

### Important Rule

The **difference between two people's ages never changes**.

Example:

A is 25 and B is 20.

Difference:

```text
25 - 20 = 5 years
```

After 10 years:

```text
A = 35
B = 30
```

Difference:

```text
35 - 30 = 5 years
```

---

## 2. Age Difference

If two people have present ages:

```text
A = 30
B = 24
```

Then:

```text
Age difference = 30 - 24
               = 6 years
```

This difference remains constant.

### Memory Rule

```text
Past       Present       Future
 ↓            ↓            ↓
Difference between ages remains constant
```

---

## 3. Past and Future Ages

If present age is `x`:

### `n` years ago

```text
x - n
```

### After `n` years

```text
x + n
```

### Example

A person's present age is 25.

5 years ago:

```text
25 - 5 = 20
```

8 years later:

```text
25 + 8 = 33
```

---

## 4. Age Ratio

Suppose:

```text
A : B = 3 : 5
```

Represent ages as:

```text
A = 3x
B = 5x
```

This is one of the most useful techniques in age problems.

### Example

The ratio of A's and B's ages is 2 : 3. Their total age is 50.

Let:

```text
A = 2x
B = 3x
```

Therefore:

```text
2x + 3x = 50
5x = 50
x = 10
```

Hence:

```text
A = 20
B = 30
```

---

## 5. Ratio After Some Years

Suppose present ages are:

```text
A = 20
B = 30
```

After 10 years:

```text
A = 30
B = 40
```

Ratio becomes:

```text
30 : 40
= 3 : 4
```

### Important

You **cannot simply add the same number to the ratio**.

Wrong:

```text
2 : 3 → 12 : 13
```

Age ratios must be calculated using actual ages.

---

## 6. Finding Ages From Sum

Suppose two people's ages are in ratio:

```text
3 : 5
```

and their total age is 64.

Let:

```text
A = 3x
B = 5x
```

Then:

```text
3x + 5x = 64
8x = 64
x = 8
```

Therefore:

```text
A = 24
B = 40
```

### Shortcut

If ratio is `a:b` and total is `S`:

```text
A = S × a/(a+b)
B = S × b/(a+b)
```

---

## 7. Finding Ages From Difference

Suppose two ages are in ratio:

```text
3 : 5
```

and their difference is 12.

Let:

```text
A = 3x
B = 5x
```

Difference:

```text
5x - 3x = 12
2x = 12
x = 6
```

Therefore:

```text
A = 18
B = 30
```

### Shortcut

If ratio is `a:b` and difference is `D`:

```text
1 ratio unit = D/(b-a)
```

## 8. Age Problems Using Equations

Many placement questions become simple equations.

### Example

A is 5 years older than B.

Let:

```text
B = x
A = x + 5
```

If their total age is 35:

```text
x + x + 5 = 35
2x = 30
x = 15
```

Therefore:

```text
B = 15
A = 20
```

---

## 9. Average Age

Average age:

```text
Average = Total age / Number of people
```

Therefore:

```text
Total age = Average × Number of people
```

### Example

Average age of 5 people is 24.

Total age:

```text
24 × 5 = 120
```

If a new person aged 30 joins:

```text
New total = 120 + 30
          = 150
```

Number of people:

```text
6
```

New average:

```text
150/6
= 25
```

## 10. Family Age Problems

Family questions often involve:

* Father and son
* Mother and daughter
* Brother and sister
* Husband and wife
* Average family age

### Example

A father is 30 years older than his son.

Let son's age:

```text
x
```

Father:

```text
x + 30
```

If their total age is 50:

```text
x + x + 30 = 50
2x = 20
x = 10
```

Therefore:

```text
Son = 10
Father = 40
```

## 11. Birth and Age Difference

Age difference remains constant.

Suppose:

```text
Father = 40
Son = 10
```

Difference:

```text
30 years
```

After 15 years:

```text
Father = 55
Son = 25
```

Difference:

```text
55 - 25 = 30
```

### Important Insight

If a parent was `D` years older than a child at the child's birth, their age difference will always be `D`.

---

## 12. Ages in Different Time Periods

Translate every statement into the same time period.

### Example

A's present age is 25.

B's age 5 years ago was equal to A's present age.

Therefore:

```text
B - 5 = 25
```

So:

```text
B = 30
```

### Another Example

A's age 5 years from now will be twice B's age 5 years ago.

Let present ages be:

```text
A = x
B = y
```

Then:

```text
x + 5 = 2(y - 5)
```

This equation can then be combined with another given condition.

## 13. Ratio and Difference

Suppose:

```text
A : B = 4 : 7
```

and their age difference is 15.

Represent:

```text
A = 4x
B = 7x
```

Difference:

```text
7x - 4x = 15
3x = 15
x = 5
```

Therefore:

```text
A = 20
B = 35
```

### General Formula

For ratio `a:b` and difference `D`:

```text
x = D/(b-a)
```

Then:

```text
A = ax
B = bx
```

---

## 14. Ratio and Sum

Suppose:

```text
A : B = 3 : 7
```

and their total age is 50.

```text
A = 3x
B = 7x
```

Therefore:

```text
10x = 50
x = 5
```

So:

```text
A = 15
B = 35
```

### General Formula

For ratio `a:b` and sum `S`:

```text
x = S/(a+b)
```

---

## 15. Age Replacement Problems

Sometimes a person's age is replaced by another person's age in an average.

### Example

Average age of 5 people is 24.

One person aged 20 leaves and another aged 30 joins.

Initial total:

```text
24 × 5 = 120
```

New total:

```text
120 - 20 + 30
= 130
```

New average:

```text
130/5
= 26
```

### Shortcut

If one person is replaced:

```text
New Average
= Old Average + (New Age - Old Age)/Number of People
```

## 16. Common Patterns

### Pattern 1: Ratio + Sum

```text
A = ax
B = bx

(a+b)x = Sum
```

### Pattern 2: Ratio + Difference

```text
A = ax
B = bx

(b-a)x = Difference
```

### Pattern 3: Present → Future

```text
Future age = Present age + n
```

### Pattern 4: Present → Past

```text
Past age = Present age - n
```

### Pattern 5: Average

```text
Total = Average × Number
```

### Pattern 6: Replacement

```text
New Total
= Old Total - Leaving Age + Joining Age
```

### Pattern 7: Age Difference

```text
Difference remains constant
```

---

## 17. Common Traps

### Trap 1: Changing Age Difference

The age difference does not change with time.

### Trap 2: Applying Years to Ratio

Wrong:

```text
3 : 5 after 10 years
= 13 : 15
```

You must first find actual ages.

### Trap 3: Mixing Time Periods

Do not compare:

```text
A's present age
```

with:

```text
B's age 10 years ago
```

without converting them to the same time period.

### Trap 4: Forgetting the Number of People

For average:

```text
Total = Average × Number of people
```

### Trap 5: Using Difference Instead of Sum

If ratio is `3:5` and total is 40:

```text
8x = 40
```

not:

```text
2x = 40
```

### Trap 6: Ignoring Integer Ages

An age problem generally expects realistic positive ages unless the question explicitly allows another interpretation.

## 18. Exam Strategy

For placement aptitude:

1. Identify the reference time: **past, present, or future**.
2. Convert all ages to the same time period.
3. Use `x` for unknown ages.
4. If a ratio is given, use `ax` and `bx`.
5. Use age difference because it remains constant.
6. For averages, convert average into total age immediately.
7. Solve the smallest possible equation.
8. Verify the final ages against every condition.

### Quick Decision Rule

```text
Ratio + Sum
    ↓
ax + bx = Sum

Ratio + Difference
    ↓
bx - ax = Difference

Average
    ↓
Total = Average × Number

Past
    ↓
Present - Years

Future
    ↓
Present + Years
```

### Final Memory Rule

```text
Age Difference → Constant

n years ago → Age - n

n years later → Age + n

Ratio a:b → ax : bx

Average → Total / Number
```
