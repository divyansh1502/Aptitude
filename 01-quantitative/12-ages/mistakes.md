# Ages — Common Mistakes

## ❌ 1. Changing the Age Difference Over Time

### Mistake

A is 10 years older than B today, so after 5 years:

```text
Difference = 15 years
```

### Correct

Both ages increase by the same amount.

```text
A = 30, B = 20
Difference = 10

After 5 years:
A = 35, B = 25
Difference = 10
```

### Avoid it

> **Age difference always remains constant.**

---

## ❌ 2. Adding Years Directly to a Ratio

### Mistake

Present ratio:

```text
3 : 5
```

After 10 years:

```text
13 : 15
```

### Why it's wrong

A ratio represents actual ages, not ages themselves.

### Correct approach

First convert the ratio into actual ages:

```text
A = 3x
B = 5x
```

Then add 10:

```text
A = 3x + 10
B = 5x + 10
```

### Avoid it

> **Never add years directly to ratio terms.**

---

## ❌ 3. Mixing Different Time Periods

### Mistake

Comparing:

```text
A's present age
```

with:

```text
B's age 5 years ago
```

without adjusting the time period.

### Correct

If A's present age is `x` and B's age 5 years ago was `y`:

```text
B's present age = y + 5
```

### Avoid it

> Put all ages on the **same timeline** before forming an equation.

---

## ❌ 4. Using Sum Instead of Difference

### Mistake

Ages are in ratio:

```text
3 : 5
```

and their difference is 10.

Using:

```text
8x = 10
```

### Correct

For difference:

```text
5x - 3x = 10
```

Therefore:

```text
2x = 10
x = 5
```

### Avoid it

```text
Ratio + Sum       → Add ratio parts
Ratio + Difference → Subtract ratio parts
```

---

## ❌ 5. Forgetting to Convert Ratio Into `x`

### Mistake

If:

```text
A : B = 3 : 5
```

treating their ages directly as 3 and 5.

### Correct

Write:

```text
A = 3x
B = 5x
```

The value of `x` represents one ratio unit.

### Avoid it

> **Ratio `a:b` → ages `ax` and `bx`.**

---

## ❌ 6. Incorrect Average-Age Calculation

### Mistake

Average age = 25 for 6 people:

```text
Total age = 25 + 6 = 31
```

### Correct

```text
Total age = 25 × 6
          = 150
```

### Avoid it

Remember:

```text
Average = Total / Number
```

Therefore:

```text
Total = Average × Number
```

---

## ❌ 7. Forgetting to Adjust Total When Someone Leaves or Joins

### Mistake

Average age of 5 people is 24. A person aged 20 leaves.

Keeping the old total unchanged.

### Correct

Initial total:

```text
24 × 5 = 120
```

After the person leaves:

```text
120 - 20 = 100
```

### Avoid it

For replacement:

```text
New Total
= Old Total - Leaving Age + Joining Age
```

---

## ❌ 8. Getting Past and Future Directions Wrong

### Mistake

For a person aged 25:

```text
5 years ago = 30
```

### Correct

Past means subtract:

```text
25 - 5 = 20
```

Future means add:

```text
25 + 5 = 30
```

### Memory Trick

```text
PAST   → -
FUTURE → +
```

---

## ❌ 9. Solving Ratio Problems Without Checking the Answer

### Mistake

You calculate ages and immediately accept them.

### Correct

Verify:

1. Original ratio
2. Given sum/difference
3. Past/future condition
4. Age difference

### Example

If you obtain:

```text
A = 20
B = 30
```

for ratio `2:3`:

```text
20 : 30 = 2 : 3 ✓
```

---

## ❌ 10. Forgetting That Everyone Ages at the Same Rate

### Mistake

After 10 years, assuming one person's age increases by 10 while another increases by a different amount.

### Correct

Every person's age increases by exactly the same number of years.

Example:

```text
A = 20
B = 30
```

After 10 years:

```text
A = 30
B = 40
```

### Avoid it

> Time passes equally for everyone.

# 🧠 Quick Mistake Checklist

Before submitting an ages answer:

* [ ] Did I identify whether the question refers to past, present, or future?
* [ ] Did I put all ages on the same timeline?
* [ ] Did I remember that age difference is constant?
* [ ] Did I convert ratios into `ax` and `bx`?
* [ ] For ratio + sum, did I add the ratio parts?
* [ ] For ratio + difference, did I subtract the ratio parts?
* [ ] Did I calculate total age correctly from average?
* [ ] Did I adjust the total when someone joins/leaves?
* [ ] Did I avoid adding years directly to ratio terms?
* [ ] Did I verify my final ages against the original conditions?
