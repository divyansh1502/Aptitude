# Ages — Practice

## 🟢 Part A — Representative Questions

### Q1. Present Age

A person's age is 24 years. What will be the person's age after 8 years?

**Solution**

```text
24 + 8 = 32
```

**Answer: 32 years**

---

### Q2. Ratio + Sum

The ages of A and B are in the ratio 3 : 5. Their total age is 64 years. Find their ages.

**Solution**

Let:

```text
A = 3x
B = 5x
```

Then:

```text
3x + 5x = 64
8x = 64
x = 8
```

Therefore:

```text
A = 24
B = 40
```

**Answer: 24 years and 40 years**

---

### Q3. Ratio + Difference

The ages of A and B are in the ratio 3 : 5. Their age difference is 12 years. Find their ages.

**Solution**

Let:

```text
A = 3x
B = 5x
```

Difference:

```text
5x - 3x = 12
2x = 12
x = 6
```

Therefore:

```text
A = 18
B = 30
```

**Answer: 18 years and 30 years**

---

### Q4. Father and Son

A father is 30 years older than his son. Their total age is 50 years. Find their ages.

**Solution**

Let son's age be `x`.

Father:

```text
x + 30
```

Total:

```text
x + x + 30 = 50
2x = 20
x = 10
```

Therefore:

```text
Son = 10
Father = 40
```

**Answer: Son = 10 years, Father = 40 years**

---

### Q5. Average Age

The average age of 5 people is 24 years. A new person aged 30 joins the group. Find the new average.

**Solution**

Initial total:

```text
24 × 5 = 120
```

New total:

```text
120 + 30 = 150
```

Number of people:

```text
6
```

New average:

```text
150/6 = 25
```

**Answer: 25 years**
