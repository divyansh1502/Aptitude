# Number System — Practice

## Part A — Representative Questions with Full Solutions

### Q1. Divisibility

What is the smallest digit `x` such that `45x6` is divisible by `9`?

**Solution**

For divisibility by `9`, the sum of digits must be divisible by `9`.

```text
4 + 5 + x + 6 = 15 + x
```

Possible values:

```text
15 + x = 18
```

Therefore:

```text
x = 3
```

**Answer: `3`**

---

### Q2. Remainder

Find the remainder when:

```text
37 × 48
```

is divided by `5`.

**Solution**

Reduce each number modulo `5`:

```text
37 mod 5 = 2
48 mod 5 = 3
```

Therefore:

```text
37 × 48 mod 5
= 2 × 3
= 6 mod 5
= 1
```

**Answer: `1`**

---

### Q3. Unit Digit

Find the unit digit of:

```text
7²⁰²
```

**Solution**

Cycle of `7`:

```text
7, 9, 3, 1
```

Cycle length:

```text
4
```

Now:

```text
202 mod 4 = 2
```

The second number in the cycle is:

```text
9
```

**Answer: `9`**

---

### Q4. Number of Factors

How many positive factors does `720` have?

**Solution**

Prime factorize:

```text
720
= 72 × 10
= 2⁴ × 3² × 5
```

Therefore:

```text
Number of factors
= (4+1)(2+1)(1+1)
= 5 × 3 × 2
= 30
```

**Answer: `30`**

---

### Q5. Trailing Zeros

How many trailing zeros are there in `125!`?

**Solution**

Count factors of `5`:

```text
⌊125/5⌋ = 25
⌊125/25⌋ = 5
⌊125/125⌋ = 1
```

Therefore:

```text
25 + 5 + 1 = 31
```

**Answer: `31`**
