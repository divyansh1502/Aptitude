# Pipes & Cisterns — Practice

## 🟢 Part A — Representative Questions

### Q1. Two Filling Pipes

Pipe A can fill a tank in 10 hours and pipe B can fill it in 15 hours. How long will they take together?

**Solution**

Rates:

```text id="c4s8ml"
A = 1/10
B = 1/15
```

Combined rate:

```text id="f1j6zk"
1/10 + 1/15
= 3/30 + 2/30
= 1/6
```

Therefore:

```text id="v9r2xd"
Time = 6 hours
```

**Answer: 6 hours**

---

### Q2. Filling + Emptying

A pipe fills a tank in 12 hours while another pipe empties it in 18 hours. If both are opened together, how long will the tank take to fill?

**Solution**

Filling rate:

```text id="g4h7cs"
1/12
```

Emptying rate:

```text id="a8k3mz"
1/18
```

Net rate:

```text id="q5p1tv"
1/12 - 1/18
= 3/36 - 2/36
= 1/36
```

Therefore:

```text id="w2n8rx"
Time = 36 hours
```

**Answer: 36 hours**

---

### Q3. Partial Filling

A pipe fills a tank in 20 hours. How long will it take to fill `3/4` of the tank?

**Solution**

Rate:

```text id="y6k2qp"
1/20 tank/hour
```

Required work:

```text id="n8f4jd"
3/4 tank
```

Time:

```text id="z1m7wc"
(3/4)/(1/20)
= 15 hours
```

**Answer: 15 hours**

---

### Q4. Leak

Leak rate:


Pipe A fills a tank in 12 hours. A and B together fill it in 8 hours. How long does B alone take?

**Solution**



