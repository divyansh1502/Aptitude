# Pipes & Cisterns — Common Mistakes

## ⚠️ 1. Adding Time Instead of Rates

### Mistake

If A fills a tank in 10 hours and B in 20 hours:

```text
10 + 20 = 30 hours ❌
```

### Correct

Convert time into rates:

```text
1/10 + 1/20
= 3/20
```

Therefore:

```text
Time = 20/3 hours
```

### Fix

> **Always add rates, never working times.**

---

## ⚠️ 2. Adding an Outlet's Rate

### Mistake

If a pipe fills at `1/10` and an outlet empties at `1/20`:

```text
1/10 + 1/20 ❌
```

### Correct

The outlet removes water:

```text
1/10 - 1/20
= 1/20
```

### Fix

> **Filling = positive, emptying = negative.**

---

## ⚠️ 3. Forgetting That a Leak Is an Outlet

A leak reduces the amount of water in the tank.

### Wrong

```text
Pipe + Leak ❌
```

### Correct

```text
Pipe - Leak
```

Example:

```text
1/8 - 1/24
= 1/12
```

### Fix

> Treat every leak as a pipe with a **negative rate**.

---

## ⚠️ 4. Using the Full Tank When Only Part Is Required

If a pipe fills a tank in 20 hours and the question asks for `3/4` of the tank:

### Wrong

```text
20 hours ❌
```

### Correct

```text
Time = (3/4) × 20
     = 15 hours
```

### Fix

> **Time = Required Work / Rate.**

---

## ⚠️ 5. Ignoring Different Opening Times

If A works for 2 hours before B joins, B did not contribute during those first 2 hours.

### Wrong

Calculate A + B for the entire duration.

### Correct

Break the problem into phases:

```text
Phase 1 → A alone
Phase 2 → A + B
```

### Fix

> Whenever a pipe opens/closes at a different time, divide the problem into phases.

---

## ⚠️ 6. Forgetting the Remaining Work

Suppose A works for 3 hours and fills `1/4` of the tank.

Remaining:

```text
1 - 1/4
= 3/4
```

Do not calculate the next pipe's time for the entire tank.

### Fix

> After every phase, update the **remaining work**.

---

## ⚠️ 7. Confusing Rate and Time

If A is twice as efficient as B:

```text
Rate(A) = 2 × Rate(B)
```

Therefore:

```text
Time(A) = Time(B)/2
```

Not:

```text
Time(A) = 2 × Time(B) ❌
```

### Fix

Remember:

```text
Efficiency ↑
Rate ↑
Time ↓
```

---

## ⚠️ 8. Not Checking Units

If:

```text
Tank = 600 litres
Rate = 50 litres/hour
```

Then:

```text
Time = 600/50
     = 12 hours
```

Do not mix litres, millilitres, minutes, and hours without conversion.

### Fix

> Make the units consistent before calculating.

---

## ⚠️ 9. Mishandling Alternate Pipes

For alternate pipes, calculate the work done in one complete cycle.

Example:

```text
A → 1 hour
B → 1 hour
```

Cycle work:

```text
A's work + B's work
```

But do **not** assume the tank must finish exactly at the end of a cycle.

### Fix

After complete cycles, check whether the tank fills during the next individual pipe's turn.

---

## ⚠️ 10. Using the Wrong Denominator

For:

```text
1/12 + 1/18
```

LCM is:

```text
36
```

Therefore:

```text
1/12 = 3/36
1/18 = 2/36
```

So:

```text
5/36
```

### Fix

> When adding rates, find the LCM carefully before combining fractions.

# 🧠 Master Memory Trick

```text
Filling Pipe → +
Emptying Pipe → -

Together → Add signed rates

Work = Rate × Time

Time = Work / Rate

Different timings → Split into phases

Leak → Negative rate

Alternate pipes → Cycle + final partial cycle
```
