# Boats & Streams — Practice

## 🟢 Part A — Representative Questions

### Q1. Basic Downstream

A boat moves at 20 km/h in still water. The stream flows at 5 km/h. Find its downstream speed.

**Solution**

```text
Downstream = Boat + Stream
           = 20 + 5
           = 25 km/h
```

**Answer: 25 km/h**

---

### Q2. Basic Upstream

A boat moves at 18 km/h in still water and the stream speed is 4 km/h. Find its upstream speed.

**Solution**

```text
Upstream = Boat - Stream
         = 18 - 4
         = 14 km/h
```

**Answer: 14 km/h**

---

### Q3. Finding Boat and Stream Speed

A boat travels downstream at 30 km/h and upstream at 18 km/h. Find the speed of the boat in still water and the speed of the stream.

**Solution**

Boat speed:

```text
B = (30+18)/2
  = 24 km/h
```

Stream speed:

```text
S = (30-18)/2
  = 6 km/h
```

**Answer: Boat = 24 km/h, Stream = 6 km/h**

---

### Q4. Distance and Time

A boat travels downstream at 24 km/h. How much time will it take to cover 72 km?

**Solution**

```text
Time = Distance/Speed
     = 72/24
     = 3 hours
```

**Answer: 3 hours**

---

### Q5. Round Trip

A boat's speed in still water is 15 km/h and the stream speed is 5 km/h. Find the total time required to travel 40 km downstream and return 40 km upstream.

**Solution**

Downstream speed:

```text
15 + 5 = 20 km/h
```

Downstream time:

```text
40/20 = 2 hours
```

Upstream speed:

```text
15 - 5 = 10 km/h
```

Upstream time:

```text
40/10 = 4 hours
```

Total:

```text
2 + 4 = 6 hours
```

**Answer: 6 hours**
