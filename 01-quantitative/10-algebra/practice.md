# Algebra — Practice

## Part A — Representative Questions

### Q1. Linear Equation

Solve:

```text
3x + 7 = 25
```

### Solution

```text
3x = 25-7
```

```text
3x = 18
```

```text
x = 6
```

**Answer: `6`**

---

### Q2. Variables on Both Sides

Solve:

```text
5x - 4 = 2x + 11
```

### Solution

```text
5x-2x = 11+4
```

```text
3x = 15
```

```text
x = 5
```

**Answer: `5`**

---

### Q3. Algebraic Identity

If:

```text
a+b=12
ab=20
```

find `a²+b²`.

### Solution

Use:

```text
(a+b)² = a²+2ab+b²
```

Therefore:

```text
144 = a²+b²+40
```

```text
a²+b² = 104
```

**Answer: `104`**

---

### Q4. Quadratic Equation

Solve:

```text
x²-7x+12=0
```

### Solution

Find two numbers whose:

* product = `12`
* sum = `-7`

They are `-3` and `-4`.

```text
x²-7x+12
= (x-3)(x-4)
```

Therefore:

```text
x=3 or x=4
```

**Answer: `3, 4`**

---

### Q5. Ratio Using Algebra

Two numbers are in the ratio `3:5`. Their sum is `64`. Find the numbers.

### Solution

Let:

```text
x=3k
y=5k
```

Then:

```text
3k+5k=64
```

```text
8k=64
```

```text
k=8
```

Therefore:

```text
x=24
y=40
```

**Answer: `24 and 40`**
