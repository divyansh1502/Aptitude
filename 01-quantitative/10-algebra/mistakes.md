# Algebra — Common Mistakes

## 1. Expanding `(a+b)²` Incorrectly

### Mistake

Writing:

```text
(a+b)² = a²+b²
```

### Why it is wrong

The middle term `2ab` is missing.

Correct:

```text
(a+b)² = a²+2ab+b²
```

### How to Avoid

Remember:

```text
First² + 2(First)(Second) + Second²
```

---

## 2. Expanding `(a-b)²` Incorrectly

### Mistake

Writing:

```text
(a-b)² = a²-b²
```

### Why it is wrong

The correct expansion contains three terms:

```text
(a-b)² = a²-2ab+b²
```

### Important

```text
(a-b)² ≠ a²-b²
```

But:

```text
(a-b)(a+b) = a²-b²
```

---

## 3. Combining Unlike Terms

### Mistake

Writing:

```text
3x + 4x² = 7x³
```

### Why it is wrong

`x` and `x²` are unlike terms.

They cannot be combined.

Correct:

```text
3x + 4x²
```

### How to Avoid

Terms can be added/subtracted only when their variables and powers match.

---

## 4. Sign Error While Moving Terms

### Mistake

From:

```text
5x+7=22
```

writing:

```text
5x=29
```

### Why it is wrong

When `+7` moves to the other side, it becomes `-7`.

Correct:

```text
5x = 22-7
```

```text
5x = 15
```

```text
x = 3
```

### How to Avoid

Think of moving a term across `=` as changing its sign.

---

## 5. Forgetting to Reverse an Inequality

### Mistake

Dividing:

```text
-2x > 10
```

by `-2` and writing:

```text
x > -5
```

### Why it is wrong

When multiplying or dividing an inequality by a negative number, the sign reverses.

Correct:

```text
x < -5
```

### Memory Rule

```text
× or ÷ by negative → Reverse sign
```

---

## 6. Cancelling Terms Instead of Factors

### Mistake

Simplifying:

```text
(x+5)/x
```

to:

```text
5
```

### Why it is wrong

You can cancel common **factors**, not individual terms connected by addition.

For example:

```text
(x×5)/x = 5
```

is valid.

But:

```text
(x+5)/x
```

cannot cancel the `x`.

### How to Avoid

Only cancel complete factors.

---

## 7. Using the Wrong Quadratic Root Formula

### Mistake

For:

```text
ax²+bx+c=0
```

forgetting the `±` sign.

### Correct Formula

```text
x = (-b ± √(b²-4ac))/(2a)
```

There can be two roots.

### How to Avoid

Always write the complete formula before substituting values.

---

## 8. Confusing Sum and Product of Roots

For:

```text
ax²+bx+c=0
```

with roots `α` and `β`:

```text
α+β = -b/a
```

and:

```text
αβ = c/a
```

### Common Mistake

Writing:

```text
α+β = c/a
```

or:

```text
αβ = -b/a
```

### Memory Trick

```text
Sum → coefficient of x with negative sign

Product → constant term
```

Both are divided by the coefficient of `x²`.

---

## 9. Treating a Ratio as Actual Values

### Mistake

If:

```text
x:y = 3:5
```

assuming:

```text
x=3
y=5
```

### Why it is wrong

The ratio tells us the relative relationship, not necessarily the actual values.

Use:

```text
x=3k
y=5k
```

Then use the additional information to find `k`.

### Example

If:

```text
x+y=40
```

then:

```text
3k+5k=40
```

```text
k=5
```

Therefore:

```text
x=15
y=25
```

---

## 10. Forgetting to Check the Original Equation

### Mistake

Solving an equation and immediately selecting the answer.

### Why it is risky

Sign errors and arithmetic mistakes can produce an incorrect value.

### Example

If you obtain:

```text
x=4
```

substitute it back into the original equation.

For:

```text
2x+3=11
```

check:

```text
2(4)+3
= 11
```

Therefore the answer is verified.

### How to Avoid

For important or tricky questions:

```text
Solve → Substitute → Verify
```
