# Time, Speed & Distance — Practice

## Part A — Representative Questions

### Q1. Basic Speed

A car travels `180 km` in `3 hours`. Find its speed.

### Solution

```text
S = D/T
  = 180/3
  = 60 km/h
```

**Answer: `60 km/h`**

---

### Q2. Distance

A train travels at `72 km/h` for `2.5 hours`. Find the distance travelled.

### Solution

```text
D = S × T
  = 72 × 2.5
  = 180 km
```

**Answer: `180 km`**

---

### Q3. Equal Distance Average Speed

A person travels equal distances at `40 km/h` and `60 km/h`. Find the average speed.

### Solution

```text
Average Speed = 2xy/(x+y)
```

```text
= 2×40×60/(40+60)
= 4800/100
= 48 km/h
```

**Answer: `48 km/h`**

---

### Q4. Meeting Problem

Two cars are `300 km` apart and move towards each other at `60 km/h` and `40 km/h`. Find the time taken to meet.

### Solution

Opposite directions:

```text
Relative Speed = 60+40
               = 100 km/h
```

Therefore:

```text
Time = 300/100
     = 3 hours
```

**Answer: `3 hours`**

---

### Q5. Train Crossing Platform

A train `200 m` long travels at `20 m/s`. It crosses a platform `300 m` long. Find the time taken.

### Solution

Total distance:

```text
200 + 300 = 500 m
```

Therefore:

```text
Time = 500/20
     = 25 seconds
```

**Answer: `25 seconds`**
